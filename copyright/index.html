Rights

Copyright and source use.

Who owns what on BRYME, and how BRYME treats other people's material.

BRYME's own work

The guides, tools, opportunity summaries and site design are BRYME's original work. Short quotations with attribution and a link are welcome. Wholesale republication is not.

How BRYME treats publications' material

An opportunity record is BRYME's own summary of a publicly available submission guideline — written in BRYME's words, not copied from the publication. Each record names and links to the original source.

BRYME does not reproduce guidelines in full, does not copy publications' page designs, and does not present another organisation's opportunity as its own.

Names and trademarks

Publication names and trademarks belong to their owners. They appear here for identification, so a writer knows which publication a record describes. Their presence does not imply endorsement, affiliation or a partnership.

Quoting other people's writing

BRYME's craft guides discuss technique using original examples. Where a real text is discussed, it is quoted only as briefly as the point requires and is attributed. BRYME does not reproduce song lyrics, poems or substantial extracts of copyrighted work.

Rights concerns

If you believe something here uses your material improperly, contact the desk with the page URL and what you would like changed. Genuine rights concerns are acted on quickly rather than argued about.
