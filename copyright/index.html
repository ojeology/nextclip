Our position

BRYME does not host video files, movies or television content. Trailers are linked or embedded from external platforms such as YouTube, and thumbnails are YouTube's generated preview images. BRYME does not claim ownership of third-party posters, trailers, screenshots or other copyrighted material — those belong to their respective owners.

Reporting potentially infringing material

If you are a rights holder and believe material on BRYME infringes your copyright, contact us with the following information:

1. The exact URL(s) on BRYME where the material appears.

2. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is affected.

3. Your relationship to the material (for example, rights holder or authorized representative).

4. Contact details so we can reply to you.

How to report

Send reports to Sodiqibrahim03@gmail.com. Every legitimate report will be reviewed, and material that is found to infringe will be removed or corrected promptly.

What happens next

BRYME reviews each report on its merits. Where a trailer or thumbnail is hosted by a third party such as YouTube, we may also point you to that platform's own reporting tools, since they control the underlying content.
