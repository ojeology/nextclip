# YouTube Video 1 — spoken script (about 7 minutes)

**Working title:** I pitched 3 websites from Lagos. Writing is not guaranteed — this is the process.

**On-screen line, first 5 seconds:**
Writing can pay. Landing the gig is not promised. This is what I actually did.

**On-screen end card:**
Full notes, dates and links: bryme.onrender.com/make-money/writing-opportunities/

Replace every `[BRACKET]` with a fact from your inbox or from the site’s own page. If you cannot fill a bracket, cut the sentence. Do not guess.

## 0:00 — Hook

I am not going to sell you a script.

I am not going to tell you writing online will change your life this week.

Writing is the only online skill that has actually paid me so far. Not because it is magic. Because I sent work to real websites, some of them replied, and some of them paid.

A lot of people in my position buy “strategies.” I would rather show you the boring version: I research a site, I write to them, I wait, and I tell you what happened.

Today I am walking through three sites I actually looked at and contacted. If you want the dates, the links and the leftover sites I checked, that lives on BRYME. But you should already understand the process by the end of this video, even if you never click.

One more time, so it is clear: **a gig is not guaranteed.** Some of these people will ignore you. That is part of the information.

## 0:50 — Who this is for

This is for people writing from a place like Nigeria, or anywhere the usual “50 sites that pay writers” list forgets.

Those lists are often American, often old, and often silent on how you actually get paid.

I am starting from Lagos. If a site only pays PayPal to a US account, that matters. If they never reply to an African email, that also matters.

If you wanted a secret that skips the writing, this is the wrong video.

## 1:20 — How I pick a site

Before I name the three, this is the filter I use.

I look for a real publication, not a Telegram “we will make you rich” page.

I look for a write-for-us page, an editor email, or a contributor form that a human might read.

I read what they actually publish. If I cannot write that kind of piece, I skip them. Pretending you can write a medical explainer when you cannot is how you burn a name.

I write down the date I applied. If they do not reply, that date is the whole story. I do not invent “they reply in two weeks” because a 2022 blog said so.

Then I send one clear pitch or one sample, depending on what they asked for. Not five follow-ups in two days.

## 5:20 — How the money actually moves

People skip this and then get angry.

First you apply. Then, if they want you, they give a brief: topic, length, deadline, and — if they are serious — how they pay.

You write the piece. They edit it. Sometimes they kill it. A killed piece is not a payment.

If they publish and they pay, the money comes through whatever method they already use. For me, from Nigeria, I care whether that method actually arrives here. I will not tell you a site “pays African writers” unless I have seen the money or they have said so in writing.

Timelines change. A site that paid in two weeks last year can ghost you this year. That is why every note on BRYME has a **last checked** date.

I also will not tell you what you will earn in a month. I do not know your English, your niche, or how many pitches you will send. Anyone who quotes you a confident monthly number is guessing, or selling.

## 6:15 — Why this beats buying a script

A script is someone else’s voice, usually written for a different country, sold to people who do not want to do the work.

This is slower. You will send emails that die. You will write samples that nobody uses.

But you will also have a sentence you can reuse: I researched this, I wrote to them on this date, this is what happened.

That sentence is how you get the next site, and the one after that.

If writing never pays you, you still practised a skill. If a bought script never pays you, you are just out of money.

## 6:40 — Close

I will keep a living list of these notes on BRYME — not two hundred sites I have never emailed. The ones I have actually checked.

If a row is wrong next month, I update the date. If I have not checked it, I say so.

Writing can pay. Landing the gig is not promised. This is the process I use instead of buying a strategy.
