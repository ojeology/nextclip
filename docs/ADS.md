# Advertising status

Advertising and third-party analytics are disabled across BRYME.

Do not add ad-network scripts or placeholders until all of the following are true:

1. the final production domain and privacy disclosures are confirmed;
2. consent requirements for each served country are implemented;
3. placements have been reviewed on mobile and desktop;
4. the page provides substantial original value; and
5. no placement can be mistaken for a job card, employer link, application button or navigation control.

Popunders, forced redirects, notification prompts and full-screen interstitials are not approved for the work publication. Job and opportunity pages must never imply that an advertiser is an employer or that clicking an ad is part of an application.

Any future rollout must begin on a small, explicitly approved route allowlist and must pass the privacy, accessibility, performance and browser release gates before deployment.
