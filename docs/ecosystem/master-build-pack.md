## SECTION 1 — THE BRYME STANDARD (GLOBAL CONSTITUTION)

**1.1 The universal editorial method:** RESEARCH → VERIFY → EXPLAIN → USE. Every page completes all four steps.

**1.2 Publication identities (never dilute):**
| Publication | Positioning | Intelligence it sells |
|---|---|---|
| Tech & AI | PRACTICAL TOOLS. NO THEATRE. | Practical technology intelligence |
| Home & DIY | FIX IT. CLEAN IT. MAINTAIN IT. UNDERSTAND IT. | Household problem-solving intelligence |
| Sport | A football information system with an editorial desk (NOT a blog) | Football intelligence |
| Entertainment | WHAT SHOULD I WATCH — AND WHY? | Viewing and entertainment intelligence |
| Fitness | Practical, safe, beginner-first fitness guidance | Practical fitness intelligence |
| Writers | Writing intelligence | Writing intelligence |

**1.3 Fact labels (mandatory on Sport and living features; encouraged everywhere):**
CONFIRMED FACT (source + date) · REPORTED (outlet + date) · RUMOURED (separate, never blended) ·
BRYME ANALYSIS (attributed to us) · BRYME PREDICTION (never presented as official data). Labels may never blur.

**1.4 Freshness standard:** every changing fact carries Last checked date + Source name/link + source's own date.
Model article: the Tech privacy piece built from official documentation.

**1.5 Source hierarchy:** 1) official documentation/league/club sources 2) primary data providers with documented
update times 3) major established media (REPORTED tier only) 4) BRYME first-hand experience (real only) 5) background only.

**1.6 Non-negotiable prohibitions:** no fabricated experience/statistics/reviews/rankings; no fake "best X" lists;
no thin/empty pages; no keyword stuffing, doorway or CPC-only pages; no misleading titles; no scraped/duplicate pages;
no fake play buttons, piracy or download pages; every page must answer "what problem does this solve?" — else merge, rewrite, noindex or retire.
