Ibrahim Sodiq writes BRYME's Make Money, Tech & AI and Sports coverage.

He learned to code largely using his phone rather than starting with an expensive computer setup, and works with Python, APIs, databases, WebSockets, automation and web development.

He has built and operated his own trading bots, including Telegram integrations, machine-learning components, databases and live dashboards, and has built websites and deployed them himself on services such as Render.

Tools he has worked with include GitHub, Firebase, Neon and PostgreSQL, Replit, Render and Capacitor. What he writes about deployment comes from troubleshooting real failures rather than from following tutorials.
