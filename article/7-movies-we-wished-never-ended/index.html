7 Movies We Wished Never Ended

Some movies end and the credits feel like a loss. These are the films we never wanted to leave.

Editorial guide · Reading time: about 2 minutes

Interstellar

The tesseract, the ghost, the fifth dimension — and then Cooper and Murph finally reunite. The film ends, and you're left wanting just one more scene with them on the porch.

Spirited Away

Chihiro leaves the spirit world, and the grass tunnel swallows her — and us. The ache of leaving that world is the whole point, and we'd walk back through the tunnel in a second.

Top Gun: Maverick

The sunset flight, the F-14, the smile between two pilots who became brothers. You want to stay in the sky with them forever.

Your Name

The stairs. The question. The tears. Mitsuha and Taki finally meet, and the credits roll — but we want to see their next hundred meetings.

Dune: Part Two

The sandworm ride, the war drums, the black-and-white arena. When it ends on Paul's war, you feel the movie is only halfway — and you'd gladly watch another four hours.

Barbie

'I'm here to see my gynecologist.' The perfect final line — and then it's over. You want to stay in Barbie Land (or at least watch Ken learn about patriarchy for another hour).

Squid Game

Gi-hun turns back at the airport, and the season ends on his choice. You need the next season immediately — and the wait makes it hurt more.
