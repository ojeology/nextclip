Solo Leveling: From E-Rank Hunter to One of Anime's Most Powerful Characters

Sung Jinwoo goes from the E-Rank hunter everyone underestimated to one of anime's most powerful characters — the double dungeon, the System, the shadow army, and why he still isn't the Shadow Monarch yet.

By Ibrahim Sodiq · Editorial guide · Reading time: about 4 minutes

When Solo Leveling begins, Sung Jinwoo is not the person you would expect to become one of the most powerful hunters in the world.

He's an E-Rank hunter.

He's weak, constantly injured and considered someone who is barely capable of surviving the dangerous world around him. While other hunters enter dungeons expecting to fight monsters, Jinwoo often enters knowing that simply making it out alive could be an achievement.

Then everything changes.

The Double Dungeon Changed Everything

The turning point comes when Jinwoo enters the mysterious double dungeon.

What starts as another dangerous dungeon quickly becomes something far worse.

The hunters discover a terrifying hidden challenge, and Jinwoo is pushed beyond his limits.

His body is brutally damaged. He is cut apart and left with almost no chance of survival.

Jinwoo dies.

But that isn't the end of his story.

He wakes up to something that nobody else has: the System.

And suddenly, the weakest hunter has something that the rest of the world doesn't understand.

The System Gives Him a Second Chance

The System changes the rules for Jinwoo.

Instead of remaining stuck at the same level forever, he can actually grow stronger.

Every battle becomes an opportunity.

Every monster becomes experience.

Every difficult fight becomes another step forward.

For someone who spent his entire life being the weakest person in the room, this is an unbelievable opportunity.

And Jinwoo takes advantage of it.

He trains.

He fights.

He levels up.

Then he does it again.

And again.

The difference between the old Jinwoo and the new Jinwoo becomes impossible to ignore.

The Weakest Hunter Starts Becoming a Monster

What makes Jinwoo's progression so satisfying is that it doesn't happen overnight.

You actually watch the transformation.

The person who once struggled to survive begins defeating enemies that would have destroyed the old version of him.

His confidence changes.

His fighting ability changes.

His appearance changes.

Even the way other characters look at him begins to change.

Eventually, the question isn't:

"Can Jinwoo survive?"

It's:

"Who can actually stop him?"

The Shadows Change Everything

Then Jinwoo's abilities become even more interesting.

His shadow army gives him something that most hunters simply don't have.

He isn't fighting alone anymore.

Every powerful enemy he defeats can potentially become part of his growing army.

Suddenly, Jinwoo can walk into a battle with an army behind him.

That's one of the most satisfying parts of watching his evolution.

The E-Rank hunter who once struggled to survive is now capable of standing in battles alongside — and eventually above — hunters who were once considered completely out of his league.

When Jinwoo Surpassed the S-Rank Level

One of the biggest signs of Jinwoo's transformation is what happens when powerful S-Rank hunters find themselves in situations they cannot handle.

Jinwoo isn't simply another hunter at this point.

He's becoming something different.

When the S-Rank hunters are pushed to the edge by an overwhelming enemy, Jinwoo is the one who arrives and changes the situation.

He even saves Cha Hae-In when she's on the brink of death.

That moment says a lot about how far he has come.

This is the same person who was once known as the weakest hunter.

Now he's saving people who were considered some of the strongest hunters in the world.

But He Isn't the Shadow Monarch Yet

This is where the anime story becomes even more exciting.

At the point we've reached in the anime, Jinwoo has become incredibly powerful, but we shouldn't confuse that with him already being the Shadow Monarch.

That transformation has not happened yet in the anime.

And that's exactly what makes the next stage of his story so interesting.

We've watched him go from E-Rank hunter to someone who has surpassed the level everyone once thought was the absolute ceiling.

But if this is only the beginning of what Jinwoo is capable of, just how far can he actually go?

What Comes Next?

This is where Solo Leveling leaves anime viewers waiting.

Jinwoo's journey isn't finished.

The future promises something much bigger than simply becoming another powerful hunter.

We're waiting to see him step fully into the power connected to the Shadow Monarch and face enemies on an entirely different level.

The battles ahead aren't just going to be about hunters and monsters anymore.

There are far greater forces waiting.

And if Jinwoo's journey from the weakest hunter to this point has taught us anything, it's this:

The Jinwoo we started with is long gone.

The real question now isn't whether he can become stronger.

It's how powerful will Sung Jinwoo become when he finally stops holding back?
