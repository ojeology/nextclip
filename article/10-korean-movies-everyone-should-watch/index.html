10 Korean Movies Everyone Should Watch

Korean cinema is the best-kept secret that isn't a secret anymore. From Oscar winners to zombie trains, here are ten films that prove Korea makes the best movies on Earth.

Editorial guide · Reading time: about 2 minutes

Parasite (2019)

The first non-English Best Picture Oscar winner. A class-war masterpiece that's funny, terrifying, and perfect.

Train to Busan (2016)

The modern zombie classic — a train ride through hell with the biggest heart in horror.

Oldboy (2003)

Park Chan-wook's revenge masterpiece. The corridor fight scene changed action cinema forever.

Memories of Murder (2003)

Bong Joon-ho's serial-killer film — the one that made his name. Haunting, funny, and unbearably sad.

The Handmaiden (2016)

A con, a heiress, and a twist you won't see coming. Park Chan-wook's most beautiful film.

Snowpiercer (2013)

The last train of humanity — a class war in one carriage at a time. Bong Joon-ho's English-language debut.

Decision to Leave (2022)

A detective falls for his suspect. The most romantic thriller of the decade — and a Cannes winner.

Burning (2018)

A slow-burn mystery that ends on one of cinema's great unanswered questions. Steven Yeun is terrifying.

A Taxi Driver (2017)

The true story of a cabbie who drove a journalist into the Gwangju Uprising. The most emotional film on this list.

The Host (2006)

Bong Joon-ho's monster movie — a creature feature about family, government failure, and ramen.
