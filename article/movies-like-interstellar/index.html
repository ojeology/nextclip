Movies Like Interstellar: Six Big Ideas to Explore Next

A spoiler-light route through six films for viewers who want more cosmic scale, emotional stakes and mind-bending questions after Interstellar.

Editorial guide · Reading time: about 2 minutes

Start with the emotion, not the science

The most satisfying follow-up to Interstellar is not necessarily another film about a spacecraft. Its real pull is the collision between an enormous idea and a personal decision. Look for stories that give their spectacle an emotional centre.

Arrival

Arrival is a strong next stop for viewers who liked Interstellar's patience with big questions. It turns language, memory and first contact into a quiet, human-scale mystery rather than a race to win.

The Martian and Gravity

For survival-focused space stories, The Martian and Gravity offer different kinds of tension. One is built around problem-solving and humour; the other stays close to immediate physical danger. Both make isolation part of the experience.

Dune: Part Two

If the appeal was scale, Dune: Part Two is a natural move. It shifts from space travel to prophecy, politics and a vast desert world, but it keeps the feeling that individual choices can reshape entire systems.

Keep the discovery going

Use this list as a starting point rather than a ranking. The best next movie depends on whether you want more wonder, more tension or more ideas to argue about after the credits.
