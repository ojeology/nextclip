Movies Like Dune: Big-Scale Science-Fiction Epics to Watch Next

Looking for movies like Dune? These sci-fi epics share the scale, the politics, or the desert-myth mood — and they are already on BRYME.

Editorial guide · Last updated 2026-08-21 · Reading time: about 2 minutes

What people mean by movies like Dune

Looking for movies like Dune usually means you want a world that feels bigger than the hero: ecology, houses, prophecy, and a camera that does not hurry. Dune (2021) is Villeneuve's first half of Frank Herbert. Dune: Part Two is the war that follows. Dune: Part Three is listed on BRYME as a 2026 title still ahead of release — not a recap, not a review of a film that is not out. This list is for the feeling of the modern Dune films, not a dump of every sand planet in cinema.

Best movies like Dune to watch next

If you have not finished the story on Arrakis, watch Dune: Part Two before you go shopping for cousins. Same desert, same director, the political bill coming due. Blade Runner 2049 is Villeneuve again: patience, huge frames, a detective instead of a duke. Rain instead of spice. The closest match in how it looks and waits. Interstellar trades houses for a family and sand for a wormhole. Pick it when the cosmic scale was the hook, not the court intrigue. Arrival is smaller and just as stubborn about language and time. Use it when Dune's ideas mattered more than the worms. The Martian is the opposite mood: jokes, engineering, a man who wants to live. Hostile planet, no prophecy. Gravity is one accident and no empire. 2001: A Space Odyssey is the cold ancestor — slow, and not a substitute for Herbert.

How to choose

Same world: Part Two. Same director's eye: Blade Runner 2049. Big ideas: Interstellar or Arrival. Survival without myth: The Martian. The 1968 argument: 2001. Start with Dune if you skipped Part One.
