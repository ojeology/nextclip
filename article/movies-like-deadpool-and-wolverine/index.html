12 Movies Like Deadpool & Wolverine

By BRYME Editorial Desk

Same DNA, same joy: meta comedy, loving violence and cameos that make a cinema shout. Twelve picks, every one with a legal place to watch.

BRYME Editorial2026-08-255 min read

If you loved the chaos, start here

Deadpool & Wolverine works because it fuses R-rated comedy with genuine superhero spectacle. These twelve films run the same spectrum — meta jokes, loving violence, and cameos that make a cinema shout.

The same DNA

Deadpool and Deadpool 2 — the originals. If you arrived at Deadpool & Wolverine through a friend, close the loop; the jokes land harder once you know the backstory.

Thor: Ragnarok — the closest Marvel ever came to full comedy before Deadpool returned. Neon, synth, Jeff Goldblum.

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 — family drama wrapped in jokes and a perfect soundtrack.

Ant-Man and Ant-Man and the Wasp — smaller stakes, heist energy, consistently funny.

The spectacle side

Spider-Man: No Way Home — the multiverse cameo-fest that proved audiences will lose their minds for legacy characters.

Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame — the two-part epic every comic film is still measured against.

Avengers: Doomsday — the new era’s opening statement.

The R-rated corner

Outside BRYME’s catalogue, the like-for-like picks are The Suicide Squad (James Gunn’s chaos-engine twin), Logan (R-rated but mournful) and Free Guy (family-friendly meta). Find where to stream every title legally from each movie’s BRYME page — we link only licensed platforms.
