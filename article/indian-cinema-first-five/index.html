Five Indian Films to Start With Across Five Different Moods

A starter route through Indian cinema in the catalogue, from large-scale action and sports drama to quiet romance and unsettling folklore.

Editorial guide · Reading time: about 2 minutes

There is no single Indian-cinema experience

The catalogue includes films in Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, each with different rhythms and traditions. This guide uses mood rather than language as the starting point.

RRR for scale

RRR is the choice when you want a large, emotionally direct action drama. Its friendship at the centre gives the spectacle a human anchor.

Dangal for a sports and family story

Dangal is a useful next watch for viewers who prefer grounded pressure over fantasy scale. The film builds its tension around training, family expectations and competition.

The Lunchbox for quiet connection

The Lunchbox moves at a gentler pace. Its mistaken delivery premise becomes a way to explore routine, loneliness and what people choose to share with a stranger.

Tumbbad and Kumbalangi Nights for a different route

Tumbbad is for viewers who want folklore and horror, while Kumbalangi Nights is for those drawn to family dynamics. Together they show why a single label cannot capture Indian cinema.
