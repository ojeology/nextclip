About Squid Game

Netflix's biggest series ever. Desperate people accept a mysterious invitation to play childhood games for a fortune — but losing means dying. A brutal satire of capitalism wrapped in pastel playground colors.

Notes

  • It became Netflix's most-watched show ever, with 1.65 billion hours in its first month.
  • The giant doll in 'Red Light, Green Light' is based on a real Korean schoolgirl character.
  • The set design hides the games in plain sight — the playground is a metaphor for the show itself.
  • The creator, Hwang Dong-hyuk, wrote the script for over a decade, and it was rejected for years.
