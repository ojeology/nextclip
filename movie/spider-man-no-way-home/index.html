About Spider-Man: No Way Home

After his identity is revealed, Peter asks Doctor Strange to make the world forget, and the spell goes wrong, pulling villains from other universes into his world. A love letter to twenty years of Spider-Man on screen.

Notes

  • The film broke pandemic box office records, grossing nearly two billion dollars.
  • The three Spider-Men were kept secret and their scenes filmed with heavy security.
  • Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield had to sign strict non-disclosure agreements.
  • The villains all return from their original films with the same actors.
