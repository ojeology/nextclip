Quick summary

Alex Garland's cosmic horror masterpiece. A biologist joins an expedition into 'the Shimmer' — a quarantined zone where DNA is being rewritten. Based on Jeff VanderMeer's novel, the film is about self-destruction, mutation, and what we become.

What Annihilation is about

Annihilation is a horror film directed by Alex Garland and released in 2018. It comes from United States, United Kingdom. The cast includes Natalie Portman, Jennifer Jason Leigh, Gina Rodriguez, Tessa Thompson. The film runs about 115 minutes.
