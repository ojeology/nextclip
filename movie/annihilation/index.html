About Annihilation

Alex Garland's cosmic horror masterpiece. A biologist joins an expedition into 'the Shimmer' — a quarantined zone where DNA is being rewritten. Based on Jeff VanderMeer's novel, the film is about self-destruction, mutation, and what we become.

Notes

  • The film was nearly shelved — Paramount tested it badly and sold overseas rights to Netflix.
  • The bear creature was made with practical effects and real bear sounds.
  • The 'Shimmer' light effects were created with real prisms and lenses, not CGI.
  • The soundtrack's unsettling choral music was composed by Geoff Barrow and Ben Salisbury.
