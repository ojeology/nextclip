About Mad Max: Fury Road

George Miller return to the wasteland after thirty years. Furiosa escapes the tyrant Immortan Joe with his five wives, and Max becomes an unwilling ally in a two-hour chase that never lets go.

Notes

  • Most of the stunts were practical, with real cars and real drivers.
  • The film won six Oscars including editing and production design.
  • It was storyboarded in detail years before filming.
  • The War Rig was built as a real vehicle with multiple camera platforms.
