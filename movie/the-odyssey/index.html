Quick summary

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, released 17 July 2026, follows Odysseus home after the Trojan War. Universal's own page calls it a mythic action epic shot on new IMAX film cameras. BRYME already had the title; this page is here because it is still the other giant in cinemas in late August, not because a trailer dropped last winter.

What The Odyssey is about

Universal Pictures published the official trailer on 22 December 2025. The billed names on that upload and on OdysseyMovie.com are Matt Damon, Tom Holland, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson, Lupita Nyong'o, Zendaya and Charlize Theron. Emma Thomas and Nolan produce for Syncopy.

BRYME is not restating every casting argument that followed the marketing. The useful fact for a reader landing now is simpler: the film opened in July and, by mid-August, was still the clear number two in North America behind Spider-Man.

Why people are talking about it

The Numbers' daily chart for 18 August 2026 had The Odyssey second in North America, with a domestic total around $513 million. Earlier August coverage from Deadline had the global run approaching a billion. Those are box-office desks, not BRYME's own count.

If you came from What's Trending, this is the adult epic still sharing IMAX with a Spider-Man movie — not a 2014 Interstellar rerun.
