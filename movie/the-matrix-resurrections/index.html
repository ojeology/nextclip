About The Matrix Resurrections

Lana Wachowski returns alone for a self-aware sequel. A game designer named Thomas Anderson starts having dreams about a world he once saved. Meta, strange, and unmistakably its own thing.

Notes

  • It is the first Matrix film directed by only one Wachowski.
  • The film openly makes fun of studio sequels and its own legacy.
  • Keanu Reeves and Carrie-Anne Moss reprise their roles after two decades.
  • The film was shot in San Francisco and Berlin.
