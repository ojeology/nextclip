About Demon Slayer: Mugen Train

The highest-grossing anime film of all time. Tanjiro and his friends board a train to hunt a demon — and fall into a dream world where the demon feeds on their deepest desires. Rengoku, the Flame Hashira, joins them for the fight of his life.

Notes

  • It grossed over $500 million worldwide — the highest for any anime film ever.
  • The film continues the anime series exactly where season 1 ends — it is episode 27 in disguise.
  • Rengoku's battle took the animation team over a year to complete.
  • The dream sequences let each character meet their lost loved ones.
