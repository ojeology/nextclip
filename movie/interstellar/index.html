About Interstellar

Interstellar is directed by Christopher Nolan and released in 2014. It comes from United Kingdom; United States. The cast includes Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Jessica Chastain, Bill Irwin. The film runs about 169 minutes.

Notes

  • Hans Zimmer wrote the score before seeing the script, based on a one-page description.
  • The wormhole and black hole visuals were generated with real physics equations.
  • The cornfield scenes were filmed in a real field that had just been harvested for the production.
  • The TARS robot was operated by puppeteers, not CGI.
