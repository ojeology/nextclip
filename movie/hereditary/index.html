Quick summary

Ari Aster's debut is the most talked-about horror film of the decade. After the matriarch dies, the Graham family unravels as dark secrets surface — miniature dioramas, a cult, and a grief that becomes possession. Hereditary is grief as horror.

What Hereditary is about

Hereditary is a horror film and released in 2018. It comes from United States. The cast includes Toni Collette, Alex Wolff, Milly Shapiro, Ann Dowd. The film runs about 127 minutes.

Notes

  • Ari Aster wrote the script after his own grandmother's death — the grief is real.
  • Toni Collette's performance is widely called one of the best horror performances ever.
  • The miniature houses in the film were built by a professional miniaturist — and mirror the real sets exactly.
  • The film was shot in sequence so the family's disintegration felt real.
