About Stranger Things

The Duffer Brothers' love letter to 80s Spielberg cinema. In the small town of Hawkins, a group of kids confronts a parallel dimension called the Upside Down, government conspiracies, and their own growing up. The biggest Netflix series of its era.

Notes

  • The show is set in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana — inspired by the Duffer Brothers' own childhood.
  • The Demogorgon costume was a real suit worn by actor Mark Steger.
  • The kids' bike-riding scenes were shot with camera rigs on actual bikes.
  • Season 1 was written to work as a miniseries — they never planned a second season.
