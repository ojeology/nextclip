Quick summary

Bong Joon-ho's Oscar-winning masterpiece. The Kim family, living in a semi-basement, schemes its way into the employment of the wealthy Park family. A pitch-black comedy, a thriller, and a class-war allegory that ends in one of cinema's great gut-punches.

What Parasite is about

Parasite is a korean film directed by Bong Joon-ho and released in 2019. It comes from South Korea, spoken in Korean. The cast includes Song Kang-ho, Lee Sun-kyun, Cho Yeo-jeong, Choi Woo-shik. The film runs about 132 minutes.
