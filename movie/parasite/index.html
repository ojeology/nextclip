Quick summary

Bong Joon-ho’s Parasite is a comedy until it isn’t, a heist until it isn’t, and a class story that refuses a sermon. The Kims need work. The Parks have a house that makes that need look like a lifestyle. BRYME’s Korean starter guide exists because a lot of people start here and then want the next five films.

What Parasite is about

The first half lets you enjoy the con. The second half charges you for it. That turn is the film. It is also why ‘movies like Parasite’ is a dangerous phrase: very little else is this precise about stairs, smell, rain, and money.

BRYME does not need to restate the Oscar night. The useful fact for a reader landing from WhatsApp is simpler: this is the Korean film most people mean, and it is darker than the thumbnail.
