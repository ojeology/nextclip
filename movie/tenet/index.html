About Tenet

Nolan inversion thriller. A secret agent learns to fight through time itself, where bullets un-fire and buildings un-explode. The most ambitious time-travel film ever attempted, and the hardest to follow on purpose.

Notes

  • The famous plane crash was a real Boeing 747 crashed for the scene.
  • The film was shot in seven countries including a real 10-minute chase.
  • Christopher Nolan reportedly wrote the script over several years to make inversion feel physical.
  • The score by Ludwig Göransson runs partly backwards in places.
