Quick summary

Nolan inversion thriller. A secret agent learns to fight through time itself, where bullets un-fire and buildings un-explode. The most ambitious time-travel film ever attempted, and the hardest to follow on purpose.

What Tenet is about

Tenet is a sci-fi film directed by Christopher Nolan and released in 2020. It comes from United Kingdom, United States. The cast includes John David Washington, Robert Pattinson, Elizabeth Debicki, Dimple Kapadia. The film runs about 150 minutes.
