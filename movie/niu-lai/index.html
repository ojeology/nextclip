Quick summary

Niu Lai (also called The Bull Comes) is a rough Chinese animation about a newborn calf, made on a tiny budget by director Xin Yumeng with his mother, Sun Lifang. It opened in China on 5 August 2026 with almost no marketing, took almost no money, then turned into a box-office event. BRYME has no official trailer to embed.

What Niu Lai is about

Reuters, writing from Beijing on 20 August 2026, described a shoestring production — hand-drawn posters, a reported cost in the thousands of dollars — that placed on China's daily chart immediately behind Spider-Man: Brand New Day after a first week of about a thousand dollars. The same report said the film's awkward animation and the story of the amateur animator and his mother became the point, not a polished studio campaign.
