About Avatar: The Way of Water

James Cameron thirteen-year sequel. Jake and Neytiri flee into the oceans of Pandora, where a new clan teaches their children to swim, breathe, and fight. A three-hour water epic that had to invent new technology to film underwater motion capture.

Notes

  • The cast trained to hold their breath for minutes to film underwater scenes.
  • Cameron waited over a decade for the technology to film underwater motion capture.
  • The film grossed over 2.3 billion dollars worldwide.
  • The water is fully computer generated around real performances.
