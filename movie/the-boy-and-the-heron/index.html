About The Boy and the Heron

Hayao Miyazaki's final film (he keeps retiring). A boy in wartime Japan follows a strange heron into a magical tower and a world between life and death. Loosely inspired by Miyazaki's own childhood.

Notes

  • It won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature in 2024 — Miyazaki's second.
  • The film took seven years to make, with Ghibli drawing by hand as always.
  • The grey heron was inspired by a real bird that visited Miyazaki's studio.
  • It is Miyazaki's most personal film — based on his wartime childhood.
