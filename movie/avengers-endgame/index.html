Notes

  • It was the highest-grossing film of all time at release, over 2.7 billion dollars.
  • The final battle featured dozens of heroes and took months of coordination.
  • The title was kept secret during production to avoid leaks.
  • The iconic portals scene was kept under wraps until the premiere.
