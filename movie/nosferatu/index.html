Quick summary

Notes

  • Bill Skarsgård's Orlok was created with prosthetic makeup and a voice lowered in post — almost unrecognizable.
  • Eggers studied 18th-century Romania and consulted historians for the film's authenticity.
  • The film was shot almost entirely in real candlelight — no digital lighting.
  • The rat plague scenes used over 5,000 real trained rats.
