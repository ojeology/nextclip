About Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga

The prequel to Fury Road, told over fifteen years. Young Furiosa is taken from her home, survives warlords, and slowly becomes the warrior who will drive the War Rig to freedom. Anya Taylor-Joy takes over the role.

Notes

  • Chris Hemsworth shaved his head and gained weight for the villain Dementus.
  • The film spans fifteen years of Furiosa life in five chapters.
  • George Miller used a vast miniature landscape for many wide shots.
  • It was shot on location in Australia over several months.
