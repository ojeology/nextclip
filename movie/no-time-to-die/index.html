About No Time to Die

Daniel Craig final Bond film. A retired Bond is called back when a scientist goes missing and a new bioweapon threatens the world. A fitting, emotional send-off for the best Bond era in decades.

Notes

  • It is the longest Bond film ever made, at 163 minutes.
  • The theme song by Billie Eilish made her the youngest artist to record a Bond theme.
  • The film had one of the longest delays in cinema history due to the pandemic.
  • It is the first Bond film to give Bond a child.
