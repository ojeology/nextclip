Quick summary

Daniel Craig final Bond film. A retired Bond is called back when a scientist goes missing and a new bioweapon threatens the world. A fitting, emotional send-off for the best Bond era in decades.

What No Time to Die is about

No Time to Die is an action film directed by Cary Joji Fukunaga and released in 2021. It comes from United Kingdom, United States. The cast includes Daniel Craig, Rami Malek, Léa Seydoux, Lashana Lynch. The film runs about 163 minutes.
