Quick summary

The modern zombie masterpiece. A workaholic father takes his daughter to see her mother in Busan — and the train ride becomes a survival nightmare when a zombie outbreak erupts. A disaster film with a heart the size of Korea.

What Train to Busan is about

Train to Busan is a korean film directed by Yeon Sang-ho and released in 2016. It comes from South Korea, spoken in Korean. The cast includes Gong Yoo, Jung Yu-mi (actress, born 1983), Ma Dong-seok, Kim Su-an. The film runs about 118 minutes.
