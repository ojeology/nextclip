About Train to Busan

Train to Busan is directed by Yeon Sang-ho and released in 2016. It comes from South Korea, spoken in Korean. The cast includes Gong Yoo, Jung Yu-mi (actress, born 1983), Ma Dong-seok, Kim Su-an. The film runs about 118 minutes.

Notes

  • It became the highest-grossing Korean film of 2016 and a global phenomenon.
  • The zombie makeup took 3 to 4 hours per extra — and hundreds of extras were used.
  • The train set was built as a full working carriage on a gimbal for the crash scenes.
  • The film's director, Yeon Sang-ho, was known for animation before this live-action debut.
