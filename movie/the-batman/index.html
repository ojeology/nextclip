About The Batman

A noir take on the Dark Knight in his second year. Batman hunts the Riddler, a serial killer targeting Gotham elite, and uncovers a corruption web that reaches the city foundations. Robert Pattinson broods like never before.

Notes

  • The Batmobile was built around a real car chassis and can actually drive.
  • The film is heavily inspired by noir classics and the Long Halloween comic.
  • The Riddler design was inspired by real serial killers and zodiac letters.
  • The Batman was filmed almost entirely at night in rainy conditions.
