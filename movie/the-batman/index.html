Quick summary

A noir take on the Dark Knight in his second year. Batman hunts the Riddler, a serial killer targeting Gotham elite, and uncovers a corruption web that reaches the city foundations. Robert Pattinson broods like never before.

What The Batman is about

The Batman is an action film directed by Matt Reeves and released in 2022. It comes from United States. The cast includes Robert Pattinson, Zoë Kravitz, Paul Dano, Jeffrey Wright. The film runs about 176 minutes.
