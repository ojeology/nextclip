About Barbie

Greta Gerwig billion-dollar meditation on womanhood, packaged as a pink comedy. Barbie leaves her perfect world, enters the real one, and brings the patriarchy question back to Barbie Land.

Notes

  • It grossed over 1.4 billion dollars, the highest for a female-directed film.
  • The film used so much pink paint that it caused a global shortage of a specific shade.
  • Margot Robbie and Ryan Gosling both earned Oscar nominations.
  • America Ferrera monologue became one of the most discussed scenes of the decade.
