Quick summary

Deadpool is pulled into a mission to save his universe and drags a reluctant Wolverine along. A love letter to Fox Marvel movies, full of cameos, chaos, and surprisingly genuine friendship.

What Deadpool & Wolverine is about

Deadpool & Wolverine is an action film directed by Shawn Levy and released in 2024. It comes from United States. The cast includes Hugh Jackman, Emma Corrin, Morena Baccarin, Rob Delaney. The film runs about 128 minutes.
