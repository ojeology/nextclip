Why no figure is printed on this page

The one publication based in Portugal prints no pay figure in its own guidelines.

Desk-wide, 171 of the 288 publications BRYME tracks state a rate. The rest are not necessarily unpaid - most simply do not publish a rate card, and BRYME will not print a number the publication has not printed itself. So treat an empty pay row as an unanswered question to put to the editor, not as evidence of anything. Where a record does carry a figure, it is quoted exactly as stated and dated with the day it was read.

For Portugal, that means the 1 record without a figure is the part of this page to test by asking — a commissioned piece is where a rate gets named.

House style in Portugal

BRYME has not separately verified a Portuguese house-style profile. Note that the journal is publishing primarily in Portuguese and English, and that European and Brazilian Portuguese differ in spelling and usage — if you are translating, follow the publication's own guideline rather than assuming a convention.
