How to pitch an essay

Adapt your pitch for personal essay and nonfiction markets: voice, the universal, and word counts.

Researched and written by BRYME Editorial Desk.

In this guide
  1. Personal essays need a voice
  2. Find the universal
  3. Be honest
  4. Match the word count
  5. Send the whole essay if asked

Personal essays are the most personal kind of pitch, and the most misunderstood.

The idea must carry a voice, not just a story. Something happened to you; what matters is the perspective you bring to it and what it says about something larger.

Find the universal in the specific. A personal essay works when a reader who is not you can still recognise themselves in it.

Be honest. Do not invent events or feelings. If you are pitching a real experience, it must be real.

Match the word count. Essay markets are often strict about length. Check the guideline before you claim a length.

Some markets want the full essay. Where the guideline says to send the complete piece rather than a pitch, follow that. Sending a pitch to a market that expects the full essay wastes everyone's time.

Desk notes: the essay pitch, dissected

Essay pitches fail differently from reported-piece pitches, so the desk separates them. A reported pitch sells access and information; an essay pitch sells a sensibility — which means the pitch itself must demonstrate the voice the essay will carry. Three sentences that do real work: the personal entry point (why you are the one who can write this), the turn (what the piece argues that a thousand think-pieces on the topic do not), and the form (length, structure, and why it suits the argument).

Where to send them: the opportunity desk records essay-friendly markets with pay and eligibility; literary-aggregator databases like Poets & Writers' filter by nonfiction. Read a market's published essays before pitching it — the pitch that quotes the section it belongs in reads like an insider's.

Craft grounding if you need it: the Purdue OWL's material on purpose, audience and stance is the formal skeleton under every good personal essay, and the desk's own essay shelf exists partly as worked examples. And keep the timing discipline: essays age fast when tied to news, slow when tied to human nature — pitch accordingly, and track it all in the tracker.

The essay pitch, worked example

To show the voice-at-work principle, here is a skeleton filled with an imaginary-but-typical pitch: "For three years I kept my grandmother's shop accounts — and watched a cash economy go digital one customer at a time" (the access: lived, specific, unreplicable by a staff writer). "This essay argues the mobile-money story everyone tells as triumph has a quiet casualty no one covers: the record-keeping knowledge that died with the ledgers" (the turn: a real argument, not a topic). "1,800 words, reported-from-memory with documents; drafts in three weeks" (the practicals). "My reporting on market traders ran in [outlet]" (the credit, or the honest absence of one). That is the entire pitch — the essay exists in seed form, and the editor can already hear its voice.

The calibration point: the seed must grow into the essay you can actually write. Overclaiming access in the pitch produces the worst kind of assignment — the one you cannot deliver. The opportunity desk records essay-friendly markets with dated guidelines; the general pitch guide covers the reported-piece variant where the physics differ.

The Pitch In Three Sentences

An essay pitch works in three sentences: the hook as an experience or argument, the shape as the piece's movement, and the proof that you are the writer. Editors buy the angle and the voice together, and the pitch is where both are visible.

  • The hook is specific A concrete moment beats a universal claim every time.
  • Show the shape Editors need to see the piece's arc before commissioning.
  • Why you The writer's stake in the subject is part of the pitch.
  • Read the outlet Pitches that reference recent pieces travel further.
  • The subject line It carries the hook or loses the inbox.
  • Follow up once One polite nudge at two weeks is professional.

Pitching is a genre with its own grammar, and the essay pitch's grammar is intimacy plus structure. Write the piece's best paragraph first and the pitch almost writes itself.
