The method

How the board is computed.

Every number here is arithmetic on the same verified results the results pages publish — nothing is modelled, predicted or scored by opinion. Clubs are ranked by points per game across the desk’s current results window (Matchweeks 1–3 for the Premier League); ties break by window goal difference, then window goals scored, then season points. Form lists the window’s results oldest to newest. Ssn is the club’s real season points, shown for context — early in a season, three good weeks are a story, not yet a verdict. The board is a BRYME desk table, clearly labelled as ours: it is not an official competition standing, and it refreshes only when the data agent verifies a new round (2026-09-11 09:25 UTC).
