The competition

How it works.

Germany’s single-division top flight is the attendance capital of Europe and the league of the 50+1 member-owned model, which keeps ticket culture cheap and the league’s identity genuinely local. Bayern Munich’s dominance defines the era; the chase behind them defines the drama.

The defending champions are Bayern Munich — a 34th title, extending the longest championship run and the most titles of any top European league’s single club. Eighteen clubs, thirty-four rounds, and the highest average attendance in world football. Like every league on this desk, promotion and relegation decide the edges of the table: the system, explained. European places flow from league position into the Champions League — and the new 36-team format changed what qualification is worth.
