Hemingway Editor alternatives

Readability tools compared — what a grade-level score actually measures, and why chasing it can make your writing worse.

Intermediate Assumes you can already draft and revise a piece.

Hemingway Editor scores your text for reading grade and highlights long sentences, passive voice and adverbs. It is genuinely useful and it is also easy to misuse, because the score is a proxy. It counts syllables and sentence lengths; it cannot tell whether you make sense. Chop every sentence into fragments and the score improves while the prose gets worse. What follows is what each tool measures and when to trust it.

Decide quickly

Which one do you actually need?

  • You want a single number for a document that must be accessible → a readability score.
  • You want to find the specific bad sentence → a sentence-length checker.
  • You are writing public-facing health, legal or government text → score it, and also test it on a person.
  • You are writing fiction or a literary essay → largely ignore the score. Long sentences are a legitimate tool.
  • Your writing is technically correct and still unclear → no tool will find this. Use a reader.

Common confusions

The questions that come up every time.

What grade level should I target?

For public-facing writing, many public bodies aim at around grade 8 to 9. For a specialist audience the number matters much less than whether the terms are glossed. Treat the score as a smoke alarm, not a grade: a sudden spike usually marks a paragraph worth rereading.

Is passive voice always wrong?

No, and tools that flag every instance are misleading. The passive is correct when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or deliberately de-emphasised — which is why scientific method sections use it. It is a problem when it hides who is responsible, which is a different thing entirely.

Can I game the score?

Easily, and people do. Split every sentence at the comma and the grade drops several levels while comprehension falls. That is the clearest demonstration that the metric is a proxy and not the goal.
