How long should you wait for an editor?

A realistic approach to editor response times and silence.

Researched and written by BRYME Editorial Desk.

In this guide
  1. Read the stated window
  2. The default two-week rule
  3. What silence usually means
  4. When to move on
  5. Keep the relationship

There is no universal answer, but good practices help.

Read the stated window first. If the guideline says “we reply within two weeks,” respect that. BRYME records stated response times and flags when they are not stated.

The default patience rule. If nothing is stated, a couple of weeks before a short follow-up is reasonable.

Understand what silence usually means. No response is rarely a personal rejection — it is usually just a busy editor. But it is also not acceptance.

When to move on. After a follow-up or two with no reply, politely treat it as a pass and pitch elsewhere.

Keep the relationship. A brief, gracious note leaves the door open for a future pitch.
