What this publication wants

This is BRYME's summary of the publication's focus — not a copy of their website. Type of writing: Fiction. Official guideline: Beneath Ceaseless Skies guidelines.

Topics, themes and styles they look for

  • Literary adventure fantasy: a secondary-world or historical-paranormal setting (up to about the 1930s), written with a literary focus on characters who yearn for something.
  • Self-standing original stories only. New and unpublished writers are welcome.
  • A short cover letter in the email body. State if the submission is simultaneous.

Subjects they avoid

  • Urban/contemporary fantasy, modern real-world settings after about the 1930s, science fiction with advanced realistic technology.
  • Reprints, novel excerpts, serials, poetry, multiple submissions.
  • Stories for which AI/LLM performed traditional elements of authorship, or where AI-based grammar check edited the text.
  • Unsolicited rewrites of rejected stories.

Key requirements

  • Email subject: Submission: (title)
  • .DOC or .RTF only — not .DOCX
  • One story at a time
  • Original, never published anywhere including a personal site

Who can submit

Official: submissions from all writers, of all locations, nationalities, ethnicities, backgrounds, faiths, genders, orientations, identities and experiences. Nigeria qualifies. No country ban.

Rights

Official: First World Serial Rights, First World Electronic Rights, non-exclusive world audio rights, non-exclusive world anthology reprint rights. After 180 days the author may reprint elsewhere.

AI policy

Prohibited

How to write for them

Official requirement: always follow the publication's own guideline exactly.

BRYME's practical advice: write for the publication's audience, use a clear structure, keep paragraphs short, lead with a specific angle, and confirm the word count before submitting. These are practical suggestions — they are not the publication's official rules.

How to pitch

Where a pitch is required, this is the general pattern BRYME recommends — always adapt it to the publication's guideline.

  1. Research the publication and read several recent pieces.
  2. Choose a specific idea that fits their beat and audience.
  3. Write a concise subject line naming your idea.
  4. Introduce yourself in one or two lines (who you are, where you publish).
  5. Explain the proposed piece in a short paragraph — angle, structure, length.
  6. Say why it fits this publication.
  7. Include 2–3 relevant clips or links.
  8. Close professionally and thank them for their time.
  9. Send through the official channel only.

What the guideline asks you to prepare

  • Email submissions@beneath-ceaseless-skies.com with subject Submission: (your title).
  • Attach a .DOC or .RTF file. Do not send .DOCX. Do not send a Google Drive link.
Safety note. Never pay to submit your work. A legitimate publication does not ask writers to pay a fee or provide identity documents to be considered. If you are asked to, do not proceed.

What happens after submission

Expected response: Average 1–3 weeks, occasionally 5–7 weeks

If the publication does not state a response time, BRYME records that it is unknown. A follow-up is generally reasonable after a couple of weeks; if there is still no response, treat that as a likely non-acceptance rather than a rejection. Acceptance is normally communicated by the publication. If they accept, they will tell you exactly how to submit the final piece and, for most, how and when payment is made.
