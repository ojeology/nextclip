Ad confirmation test

This page exists only to prove the advertising pipeline on your device. If the system works you will see, within about 30 seconds: a labelled ad slot below — and a small dark diagnostic box with green text in the bottom-left corner ending in a plain-English verdict.

If the green box says “FILL DETECTED” — ads work. If it says the provider sent no ad — the zones are the issue. If no box appears at all, something on this device or network is blocking the site’s scripts (ad blocker, AdGuard DNS, or a proxy browser such as Opera Mini).
