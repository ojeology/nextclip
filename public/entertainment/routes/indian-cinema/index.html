INDUSTRY ROUTE · INDIA

Indian cinema

Many industries, many languages — the largest film output on earth.

55 titles from this industry are in the hand-verified BRYME catalogue, years 1955 to 2026, each entry carrying an official trailer checked against YouTube’s oEmbed record. This is a shelf, not an encyclopedia: what is listed here is what the desk has actually verified.

Directors appearing more than once: S. S. Rajamouli, Aditya Dhar, Sukumar.

The shelf

Every title, newest first

  • Vishwanath and Sons (2026) · Drama
    Suriya plays Sanjay Vishwanath, a pistol shooter still chasing a late career.
  • Jana Nayagan (2026) · Action
    Thalapathy Vijay's Tamil action film for KVN Productions, directed by H. Vinoth.
  • Cocktail 2 (2026) · Romance
    Homi Adajania returns to the Cocktail title with Shahid Kapoor, Kriti Sanon and Rashmika Mandanna. A new triangle, not a shot-for-shot of the 2012 film.
  • Dhurandhar (2025) · Action
    Aditya Dhar’s spy-action film with Ranveer Singh. Akshaye Khanna and Arjun Rampal are billed in the opposition. A Hindi espionage picture, not a biopic claim.
  • Article 370 (2024) · Thriller
    A Delhi-based agent is sent into Kashmir around the 2019 constitutional change that ended the state’s special status.
  • Pushpa 2: The Rule (2024) · Action
    Pushpa Raj is no longer climbing. The sequel to The Rise puts him in a fight with the state, a rival syndicate, and a cop who will not drop the file.
  • Kalki 2898-AD (2024) · Sci-Fi
    Kasi in the year 2898 is a desert city waiting for a prophecy.
  • Stree 2 (2024) · Horror
    Chanderi has a new night problem, and the same group of friends who survived the first Stree are not ready.
  • Bad Newz (2024) · Comedy
    A messy love triangle sold as a comedy. Vicky Kaushal, Triptii Dimri and Ammy Virk. Anand Tiwari’s 2024 Hindi film, not a news satire.
  • Bhool Bhulaiyaa 3 (2024) · Horror
    Kartik Aaryan walks into another haunted palace; Vidya Balan and Madhuri Dixit are already inside the joke.
  • Devara: Part 1 (2024) · Action
    N. T. Rama Rao Jr. in a coastal-action saga from Koratala Siva. Saif Ali Khan and Janhvi Kapoor are billed opposite him. Part 1 of a two-film story.
  • Munjya (2024) · Horror
    A Konkan wedding, a family curse, and a spirit who will not stay in the tree. Aditya Sarpotdar’s horror-comedy with Abhay Verma and Sharvari. Hindi, 2024.
  • Singham Again (2024) · Action
    Rohit Shetty’s cop-universe pile-up. Kareena Kapoor Khan, Arjun Kapoor and Jackie Shroff are billed alongside the Singham brand.
  • Yodha (2024) · Action
    Siddharth Malhotra in an airborne action film. Raashii Khanna and Disha Patani are billed. A 2024 Hindi thriller set around a hijack, not a mythological epic.
  • Jawan (2023) · Indian
    A vigilante and his father wage war on corruption across India. Shah Rukh Khan, Atlee.
  • Pathaan (2023) · Indian
    A disavowed Indian agent returns to stop a private terror group. Shah Rukh Khan's mega-comeback.
  • Animal (2023) · Action
    A son who cannot sit still in his father’s shadow answers a hit with a war.
  • Kantara (2022) · Action
    A village conflict over land and tradition grows into a larger story shaped by local ritual and belief.
  • KGF: Chapter 2 (2022) · Indian
    Rocky rises to rule the Kolar Gold Fields — and takes on the whole country. Yash.
  • RRR (2022) · Action
    Two men with opposing public roles forge a powerful friendship during colonial rule in India.
  • Vikram (2022) · Action
    Lokesh Kanagaraj’s Tamil action film. Kamal Haasan’s title role sits opposite Vijay Sethupathi and Fahadh Faasil. A 2022 hit, not a remake of the 1986 Vikram.
  • Pushpa: The Rise (2021) · Indian
    A daily-wage laborer becomes the king of red sandalwood smuggling. Allu Arjun.
  • The Great Indian Kitchen (2021) · Drama
    A newly married woman confronts the repetitive labour and unequal expectations built into her household life.
  • Article 15 (2019) · Crime
    A police officer investigates the disappearance of young women in a village and confronts entrenched caste discrimination.
  • Gully Boy (2019) · Drama
    A young man from Mumbai finds a voice through rap while navigating family pressure and social expectations.
  • Jallikattu (2019) · Thriller
    When a buffalo escapes into a village, an attempt to capture it exposes fear, rivalry and crowd instinct.
  • Kabir Singh (2019) · Indian
    A brilliant surgeon destroys his life and career over a broken heart. Controversial and enormous.
  • Kumbalangi Nights (2019) · Drama
    Four brothers living in a coastal village must confront their strained relationships when new people enter their lives.
  • Super Deluxe (2019) · Drama
    Several lives intersect across one extraordinary day as families confront secrets, judgement and chance.
  • Uri: The Surgical Strike (2019) · Indian
    The true story of India's 2016 surgical strike across the Line of Control.
  • Andhadhun (2018) · Thriller
    A pianist becomes entangled in a murder case after witnessing something he was never meant to see. The thriller plays with perception and deception.
  • Stree (2018) · Indian
    A town haunted by a witch who only takes men who don't respect women. Indian horror-comedy done right.
  • Tumbbad (2018) · Horror
    A man’s search for a hidden fortune leads him deeper into a dangerous family legend.
  • Baahubali 2: The Conclusion (2017) · Indian
    The answer to the greatest question in Indian cinema — and the conclusion of the epic.
  • Vikram Vedha (2017) · Crime
    A police officer hunting a gangster is drawn into stories that challenge his simple idea of right and wrong.
  • Dangal (2016) · Drama
    A former wrestler trains his daughters to compete in a sport rarely open to them in their community.
  • Sairat (2016) · Romance
    Two young people from different social backgrounds fall in love and try to build a life away from the expectations around them.
  • Baahubali: The Beginning (2015) · Fantasy
    A young man discovers a kingdom’s history and his connection to a struggle for power. The fantasy adventure is built around mythic scale.
  • Bajrangi Bhaijaan (2015) · Indian
    A devout Hanuman devotee crosses the border to return a mute Pakistani girl to her family. Salman Khan's best.
  • Masaan (2015) · Drama
    Two stories unfold in Varanasi as young people confront grief, judgement and the possibility of a new beginning.
  • Court (2014) · Drama
    A folk singer faces prosecution after his work is linked to a worker’s death. The courtroom drama observes institutions and everyday injustice.
  • Haider (2014) · Drama
    A young man returns to Kashmir to search for his missing father and becomes caught between grief, politics and revenge.
  • Kaaka Muttai (2014) · Comedy
    Two brothers from a Chennai neighbourhood become determined to taste pizza after a new restaurant opens nearby.
  • PK (2014) · Indian
    An alien stranded on Earth questions the world's religions. Aamir Khan's comedy-drama.
  • Dhoom 3 (2013) · Indian
    A circus acrobat turns to robbery to avenge his father. Aamir Khan, Aishwarya Rai.
  • Drishyam (2013) · Thriller
    A family man constructs an elaborate alibi to protect his family after a tragic incident. The thriller centres on memory, pressure and consequence.
  • Queen (2013) · Comedy
    After her wedding is called off, a young woman takes the planned honeymoon alone and discovers new confidence through travel.
  • The Lunchbox (2013) · Drama
    A mistaken lunch delivery begins a private exchange between two strangers in Mumbai.
  • Gangs of Wasseypur (2012) · Indian
    Three generations of a coal-mining family wage war over Wasseypur. Anurag Kashyap's crime epic.
  • Udaan (2010) · Drama
    A teenager is forced to return home and live under his strict father’s control while trying to pursue his own ambitions.
  • 3 Idiots (2009) · Comedy
    Three engineering students challenge the pressure and expectations around academic success.
  • Awarapan (2007) · Romance
    Mohit Suri’s 2007 Hindi noir. Emraan Hashmi plays a man who does other people’s violence until it comes home. Shriya Saran is billed opposite him.
  • Lagaan (2001) · Drama
    Villagers facing a crushing colonial tax challenge their rulers to a cricket match despite having little experience of the game.
  • Sholay (1975) · Action
    Two small-time criminals are hired to protect a village from a ruthless bandit. It is a large-scale action drama with comedy and friendship at its core.
  • Pather Panchali (1955) · Drama
    A family in rural Bengal faces hardship while two children discover the world around them. It is an intimate coming-of-age drama.
