Writing opportunities in South Africa

1 publication is based in South Africa, and 82 in total confirm in their own guidelines that they are open to writers from here. A further 46 do not state an eligibility rule either way.

Pay below is shown exactly as each publication states it. For scale, US$100 is about R1,597 at the mid-market rate of 2026-09-05. BRYME never converts a stated rate — it only shows an approximation.

A listing is an invitation to pitch, never a promise of acceptance or payment. Every entry links to the publication’s own guideline and the date BRYME last checked it.
