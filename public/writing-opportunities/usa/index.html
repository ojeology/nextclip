House style when you write for United States

Getting these wrong will not sink a good idea, but they are the fastest way for an editor to tell you have not read them.

Spelling-ize and -or: organize, color, honor, labor
DatesApril 3, 2026 — month first, comma after the day
CV or résuméRésumé, usually one page, never with a photo
QuotationDouble quotes, and commas and full stops go inside them

The largest market BRYME tracks, and the one most likely to state a per-word rate up front.
