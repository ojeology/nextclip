HOW WE WORK4 RULES

Same four rules on every desk

We check before we publish

Primary source first — government page, company's own docs, publication's own guidelines. Secondary sources find primary, never replace it.

We date anything that can go stale

Prices, rules, schedules change. Every page that could age carries last-checked date.

We separate what we know from what we think

Research, firsthand experience, analysis labelled differently. Where we submitted, we say so.

We publish our mistakes

Corrections in open, kept on public log. A publication that never corrects isn't careful — it's not looking.

What you will never find: pop-ups, interstitials, autoplaying video · earnings we cannot verify · copy taken from another site · medical/legal/financial advice dressed up as fact
