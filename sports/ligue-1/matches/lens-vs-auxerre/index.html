Match overview

Lens host Auxerre at the Stade Bollaert-Delelis on the opening weekend, a fixture between two clubs whose supporters treat a Saturday afternoon in the north as something closer to an obligation than an entertainment. Bollaert is one of the few grounds in France where the atmosphere is a genuine variable rather than a cliché, and Auxerre arrive knowing the first twenty minutes will be loud.

The case for the underdog

Auxerre travelling to Bollaert on an opening weekend is the harder half of this fixture, and their route to a result is the one every away side takes there: survive the opening period, quieten the ground, and make the home crowd anxious rather than loud.
