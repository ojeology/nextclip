Match overview

Troyes are back in Ligue 1 and open at home to Paris FC at the Stade de l'Aube. Two clubs with something to prove: Troyes promoted alongside Le Mans, Paris FC still building an identity distinct from the club that shares their city and owns the sport's attention. There is no glamour in this fixture and a great deal riding on it.

Recent form

Matchday one. Troyes arrive as one of the two promoted clubs.

Historical context

ESTAC Troyes and Le Mans FC are the clubs promoted from Ligue 2 for the 2026/27 season, which runs from 21 August 2026 to 29 May 2027 across 34 matchdays.

The case for the underdog

This is the rare opening fixture with no obvious underdog, which makes it more interesting than the scoreline will probably suggest. Both sides will consider a point acceptable and a defeat disproportionately damaging — and games played on that basis are usually cautious.

BRYME editorial outlook

BRYME expects a cagey game between two teams who both need the season to start well and neither of whom can afford to lose at home or away in week one.
