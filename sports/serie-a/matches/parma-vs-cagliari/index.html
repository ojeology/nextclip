Match overview

Parma host Cagliari at the Stadio Ennio Tardini in the second of the two 20:45 fixtures on Serie A's opening Saturday. Both clubs spent last season closer to the bottom half than the top, and both will privately regard the other as the sort of opponent whose points decide whether a season is comfortable or anxious. Opening fixtures between likely mid-to-lower table sides are worth more than the table suggests in August.
