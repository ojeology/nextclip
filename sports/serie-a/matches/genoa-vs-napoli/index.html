Match overview

Genoa host Napoli at the Stadio Luigi Ferraris in the evening slot on Serie A's opening Saturday. The Ferraris under lights on a first matchday is one of the better atmospheres in Italian football, and Napoli — expected to be among the season's leading contenders — arrive knowing that an away opener in Genoa is a genuine test rather than a formality.

Historical context

Napoli's season is shaped early: they face Inter at San Siro on 5 September in what is expected to be the first meeting of two title contenders. That fixture makes the opening two matchdays more consequential than they look — arriving at Inter having dropped points in Genoa would change the framing of Napoli's campaign before September is out.

The case for the underdog

Genoa are the underdog and the Ferraris is their argument. Away favourites on opening night in Italy have a long history of being frustrated by exactly this kind of fixture: a hostile ground, a well-organised home side, and an opponent still finding its rhythm.
