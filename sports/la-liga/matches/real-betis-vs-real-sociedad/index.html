Historical context

Barcelona won La Liga in 2025/26 with 94 points from 38 games — 31 wins, one draw, six defeats, 95 goals scored. Real Madrid finished second on 86, Villarreal third on 72, Atlético Madrid fourth on 69. Betis's fifth place came with an unusual profile — 15 wins and 15 draws from 38 games, the most drawn season of any side in the division.

The case for the underdog

Real Sociedad are the lower-placed side from last season and away from home, but a first fixture of a season played by two teams who have both been waiting a week is not a normal away game. Neither side has rhythm; neither has a settled XI on show. That flattens the home advantage more than usual.

BRYME editorial outlook

Betis at home should edge it, but their fifteen draws last season are hard to ignore. BRYME would not be surprised by a point apiece and would be mildly surprised by a comfortable win either way.
