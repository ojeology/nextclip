Where to Watch the Premier League in Nigeria (2026)

Showmax, DStv or GOtv? The honest guide to legally watching every Premier League match from Nigeria this season — with estimated prices and data-saving tips.

BRYME Editorial2026-08-255 min read

The legal options that matter

Every Premier League match shown in Nigeria runs through a small number of rights holders. Here is the honest state of play for the 2026/27 season — prices are estimates as of August 2026 and change with promotions, so confirm on each official page before paying.

Showmax — the football-specific option

Showmax’s mobile plan built around Premier League football is the cheapest full-season route for most Nigerian fans, estimated around ₦3,200 a month on mobile. It also carries the local drama and reality catalogue, which makes it the all-rounder for a Nigerian household.

DStv and GOtv — the full SuperSport package

Multichoice’s SuperSport channels remain the widest sports coverage in the country — every kickoff, studio shows and the other big leagues — at a higher monthly price (streaming tiers run well past ₦10,000). GOtv is the cheaper option with a reduced channel set.

What about StarTimes and YouTube?

StarTimes ON carries plenty of football — Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and the 2026 World Cup — but not the Premier League. YouTube’s official Premier League channel is the home of free, legal highlights a few hours after each game.

Four ways to save data while streaming

  • Use the mobile plan’s lower data profile — it is engineered for Nigerian 3G/4G networks.
  • Watch official highlights over wifi instead of full matches on mobile data.
  • Set stream quality manually to 480p when on cellular.
  • Check BRYME’s verified results and tables first — sometimes the score is all you needed.

BRYME links only legal, licensed sources. If a site streams Premier League matches for free with no rights notice, it is not legal — and it is not worth your data.
