Premier League Matchweek 2: Fixtures, Times & Picks

Ten fixtures, four days, and kick-off times that read the same in Lagos as in London. Every Matchweek 2 game, the storyline attached to it, and what to watch first.

BRYME Editorial2026-08-255 min read

The weekend at a glance

Matchweek 1 gave us Brighton putting four past Aston Villa, Hull City out-playing Manchester United, a Xabi Alonso winning start at Chelsea and Manchester City leaving it late against Bournemouth. Matchweek 2 spreads the drama over four days — and because British Summer Time and West Africa Time sit on the same clock this month, every kick-off below is Lagos time.

Three games we would not miss

Chelsea v Brighton. A genuine top-of-the-table Sunday clash: two teams that scored four and three respectively last weekend, and the league’s two in-form attackers in Morgan Rogers and Jack Hinshelwood.

Crystal Palace v Man City. Friday night, a raucous Selhurst Park, and the first proper look at how City control games without Rodri. Palace will sit deep and ask the new midfield to unlock them.

Aston Villa v Arsenal. Monday night closes the round with the biggest fixture on paper — a wounded Villa against an Arsenal rebuilt at the back with Konsa and Meslier.

One early pick per game (editorial)

City win at Palace; Liverpool win; Bournemouth draw Everton; Coventry v Hull ends level; Spurs edge Newcastle; Chelsea edge Brighton; Brentford beat Leeds; Sunderland beat Fulham; Man Utd beat Ipswich; Arsenal draw at Villa.
