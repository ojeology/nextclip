Privacy Policy

Clear and honest information about privacy on BRYME.

Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact BRYME by email or WhatsApp, you voluntarily provide the information contained in your message (such as your email address and the content of your enquiry). BRYME uses that information only to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

BRYME is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process standard technical data (such as IP addresses and request logs) as part of operating the service. BRYME itself does not run analytics tracking on its pages.

Cookies and local storage

BRYME does not set its own cookies for advertising or tracking, and does not rely on local storage for personal data. Standard browser mechanisms may operate as part of normal web use, but BRYME does not read or store personal information through them.

Analytics

BRYME does not currently use Google Analytics or any other analytics service. If analytics is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe it.

External services and YouTube

Trailers are embedded from YouTube using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. When you play a trailer, YouTube's own privacy policy and cookie practices apply. BRYME has no control over YouTube's data handling.

Links to third-party websites (such as streaming platforms) leave BRYME; those websites have their own privacy policies.

Advertising

BRYME does not currently display advertising and does not use advertising partners or advertising SDKs. If advertising or sponsored content is introduced later, it will be clearly identified and this policy will be updated.

How information may be used

Information you send us is used to respond to your enquiry, improve the website and address reported issues. BRYME does not sell personal information.

Data retention

Correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry. BRYME does not maintain user accounts or store visitor profiles.

Your rights

You may contact BRYME at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request correction or deletion, or to ask questions about this policy.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page.
