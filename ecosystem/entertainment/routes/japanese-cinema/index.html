INDUSTRY ROUTE · JAPAN

Japanese cinema

Animation and live action on one shelf, tracked by studio and era.

87 titles from this industry are in the hand-verified BRYME catalogue, years 1950 to 2025, each entry carrying an official trailer checked against YouTube’s oEmbed record. This is a shelf, not an encyclopedia: what is listed here is what the desk has actually verified.

Directors appearing more than once: Hayao Miyazaki, Makoto Shinkai, Haruo Sotozaki.

  • Shoplifters (2018) · Drama

    A man reincarnates as a slime with the power to absorb anything — and builds a monster nation.
  • Violet Evergarden (2018) · Anime

  • My Hero Academia (2016) · Anime
    In a world where 80% of people have quirks, Izuku Midoriya inherits the power of the world's greatest hero and enters U.A. High.
  • Re:Zero – Starting Life in Another World (2016) · Anime
    Subaru is transported to a fantasy world with one power: when he dies, time rewinds and he remembers everything.

    Goku and Vegeta reach god-level power against Beerus, Zamasu and the Tournament of Power.

    Saitama can defeat any enemy with a single punch — and he's bored. The comedy-action masterpiece from ONE and Yusuke Murata.

    A short volleyball player with a huge jump joins his school's team and aims for nationals.

  • JoJo's Bizarre Adventure (2012) · Anime

  • Kuroko's Basketball (2012) · Anime
    The phantom sixth man of a legendary team returns to high school basketball with a new teammate.
  • Sword Art Online (2012) · Anime
    Players trapped in a VR death game must clear all 100 floors to escape — or die in real life.
  • Hunter x Hunter (2011) · Anime
    Gon Freecss becomes a Hunter to find his father — and encounters an impossible world of Nen, Chimera Ants and more.

    A self-proclaimed mad scientist accidentally invents time travel and must face the consequences of changing the past.

    The wizards of the Fairy Tail guild take on impossible jobs — with friendship as their real power.

    A genius student gets a power that lets him command anyone — and uses it to overthrow an empire.
  • Gintama (2006) · Anime
    An alien-invaded Edo era, a lazy samurai, and the most chaotic comedy in anime.
  • Paprika (2006) · Anime
    A therapist uses a device that lets her enter dreams — until it's stolen. Satoshi Kon's masterpiece.
  • Bleach (2004) · Anime
    Ichigo Kurosaki becomes a Soul Reaper and defends the living world from evil spirits called Hollows.
  • Howl's Moving Castle (2004) · Anime
    A young woman cursed into old age finds refuge in a wizard's magical walking castle. Miyazaki.
  • Samurai Champloo (2004) · Anime
    A waitress hires two swordsmen to find 'the samurai who smells of sunflowers' — hip-hop meets Edo Japan.
  • Naruto (2002) · Anime
    The classic shonen anime by Masashi Kishimoto. Naruto Uzumaki, the village outcast, trains to become Hokage while chasing his rival Sasuke — a story of ninjas,…
  • Spirited Away (2001) · Anime · BRYME 10/10
    A girl wanders into a spirit world and must work in a bathhouse to save her parents — and herself.
  • Battle Royale (2000) · Thriller
    A class of students is forced into a deadly state-sanctioned contest on a remote island. The thriller examines authority, fear and survival.
  • One Piece (1999) · Anime
    The legendary anime based on Eiichiro Oda's manga. Monkey D. Luffy sets sail to find the One Piece and become King of the Pirates, gathering a crew of dreamers…
  • Cowboy Bebop (1998) · Anime
    Bounty hunters in space, a jazz soundtrack, and one of the greatest endings in anime. 'See you space cowboy.'
  • Trigun (1998) · Anime
    The pacifist gunslinger Vash the Stampede, worth sixty billion double-dollars, is hunted across a desert planet.
  • Princess Mononoke (1997) · Anime · BRYME 9/10
    A prince, a wolf-girl, and a war between humans and the forest. Miyazaki's greatest epic.
  • Perfect Blue (1997) · Animation
    A pop idol leaves music for acting and begins to question what is real as pressure and obsession close in.

    A delinquent joins his school's basketball team for a girl — and discovers a passion.
  • Porco Rosso (1992) · Anime

  • Dragon Ball Z (1989) · Anime
    Goku defends Earth from increasingly cosmic threats — Frieza, Cell, Majin Buu. The most famous shonen of all time.
  • Kiki's Delivery Service (1989) · Anime
    A young witch leaves home to start her own delivery service in a seaside city.

  • Grave of the Fireflies (1988) · Anime

    A boy and a girl search for the legendary floating city of Laputa. Ghibli's first masterpiece.
  • Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind (1984) · Anime
    A princess fights to save her valley and the poisoned world from war and the toxic jungle.

    Several witnesses describe the same violent event in conflicting ways. The film is a classic study of memory, perspective and truth.
