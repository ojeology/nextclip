What to watch next

Recommendations, starter routes and watch orders — the answer to “what should I watch tonight?”, argued honestly rather than scraped.

Everything on this shelf is picked by the desk against stated criteria — tone, structure, how easy a thing is to watch — never against a licensing deal or a commission. We deliberately do not claim where something is streaming: catalogues rotate by country and month, so the desk’s job is to tell you why a title is worth your night, and the where is a search you run on the evening. Rankings here are arguments, not queues; skip to the entry that matches your mood.
