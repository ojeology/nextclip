The Manchester City corner

City broke their transfer record mindset for Elliot Anderson and are reportedly closing on Lille teenager Ayyoub Bouaddi as the Rodri succession plan, with Liverpool’s Cody Gakpo also on the radar. Outgoing: Rodri to Barcelona, James Trafford to Leeds, plus loans for Divine Mukasa and Emilio Lawrence. Kalvin Phillips arrived on loan from Sheffield United.

Still in play as deadline day nears

  • Carlos Baleba — Brighton to Manchester United, full agreement reported.
  • Bradley Barcola and Enzo Fernández — both reportedly edging toward moves.
  • Barış Alper Yılmaz — Galatasaray to Everton, offer around £35m reported on the table.
  • Samuel Chukwueze — AC Milan to Fulham, advanced talks reported.
  • Raphaël Le Guen — Brest to Crystal Palace, personal terms reportedly agreed.
  • Gabriel Martinelli — a Saudi move reported as negotiations continue.
