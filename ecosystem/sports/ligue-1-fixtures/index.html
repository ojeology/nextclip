The 2026-27 Ligue 1 calendar.

Calendar: Official LFP 2026/27 calendar (published 10 June 2026); match dates/times from the ESPN Ligue 1 feed, validated against the official LFP round windows and the official broadcast programmes for rounds 1–3 · official source · desk file last updated 14 August 2026 · Kick-off times are CET/CEST (France). The LFP confirms kick-off times roughly three weeks before each round: rounds 1–3 are confirmed; later rounds show 'TBC' until the LFP publishes the times. Typical slots: Friday 20:45/21:05, Saturday 17:15/20:45, Sunday 15:00/17:15/20:45.

Fixtures and kick-off times are subject to change (TV selections, European competitions, Coupe de France). BRYME updates the list as official changes are announced. Rounds appear here as scheduled fixtures; results are added only after the desk verifies them — an unverified result is never published.
