The 2026-27 La Liga calendar.

Calendar: Official LaLiga 2026/27 calendar; dates and kick-off times as compiled from the ESPN LaLiga feed (which follows official LaLiga announcements, including the 13 August 2026 Celta–Osasuna postponement) · official source · desk file last updated 14 August 2026 · Kick-off times are CET/CEST (Spain). LaLiga confirms kick-off times round by round, roughly three weeks in advance: Jornadas 1–4 are confirmed; later rounds show 'TBC' until LaLiga publishes the times.

Fixtures are subject to change (TV selections, European competitions, Copa del Rey). BRYME updates the list as official changes are announced. Rounds appear here as scheduled fixtures; results are added only after the desk verifies them — an unverified result is never published.
