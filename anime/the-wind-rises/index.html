What The Wind Rises is about

The Wind Rises is a drama, historical and romance anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 2013. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Hideaki Anno, Miori Takimoto, Hidetoshi Nishijima, Masahiko Nishimura. The anime runs about 126 minutes.
