Quick summary

Hayao Miyazaki's masterpiece and the only anime film ever to win the Oscar for Best Animated Feature. Ten-year-old Chihiro enters a world of gods and spirits, loses her name, and must find her way home.

What Spirited Away is about

Spirited Away is a fantasy, family and adventure anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 2001. It comes from Japan, spoken in Japanese. The cast includes Rumi Hiiragi, Miyu Irino, Mari Natsuki, Yasuko Sawaguchi. The anime runs about 125 minutes.

Notes

  • It is the only non-English-language film ever to win the Best Animated Feature Oscar.
  • Miyazaki wrote the script after meeting his friend's 10-year-old daughter.
  • The bathhouse was inspired by an actual bathhouse in Tokyo that Miyazaki visited as a child.
  • Studio Ghibli drew the entire film by hand — over 1,400 animation cels.
