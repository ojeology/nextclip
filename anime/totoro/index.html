What My Neighbor Totoro is about

My Neighbor Totoro is a family and fantasy anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 1988. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Noriko Hidaka, Chika Sakamoto, Hitoshi Takagi. The anime runs about 86 minutes.
