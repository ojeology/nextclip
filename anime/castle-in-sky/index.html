What Castle in the Sky is about

Castle in the Sky is an adventure, fantasy and family anime directed by Hayao Miyazaki and released in 1986. It comes from Japan. The cast includes Mayumi Tanaka, Keiko Yokozawa, Minori Terada. The anime runs about 124 minutes.
