Blog readers skim. A post either earns their attention fast or loses it. These dos and don'ts are what makes a post read to the end.

## Do

- **Do write a specific, promise-y headline.** "How to stop wasting the first hour of your writing day" names a problem and a benefit. Skimmers need that hook.
- **Do get to the point.** A brief introduction that puts the reader in the problem. Skip the long preamble about writing.
- **Do structure with headings and short paragraphs.** Use headings, bullets, and short blocks so a skimmer can get the gist. Walls of text lose readers.
- **Do give a concrete takeaway.** A named, specific plan or number the reader can act on beats generic advice.
- **Do end with a next step.** A call to action — a related guide, a question, a subscription.
- **Do make it genuinely useful.** A post that solves a real problem gets read and shared; one that is thin filler does not.

## Don't

- **Don't use a vague headline.** "Writing Tips" or "Thoughts on blogging" gives the reader no reason to open.
- **Don't pad with an intro that goes nowhere.** "In today's fast-paced world, writing is more important than ever" — cut it. Start with the problem.
- **Don't write walls of text.** Long paragraphs without breaks are hard to scan and lose mobile readers.
- **Don't stuff it with generic advice.** "Write clearly, be consistent, know your audience" — everyone can say that. Be specific and original.
- **Don't keyword-stuff.** Writing for search engines instead of people produces unreadable posts that rank poorly and please no one.
- **Don't forget a point.** A post that says nothing useful is not a post worth keeping.

## The quick test

Could a reader skim only the headlines and bullets and still get the main idea and the takeaway? If yes, the structure works. If no, add clearer headings.

## Checklist

- [ ] My headline is specific and promises a benefit.
- [ ] I got to the point quickly.
- [ ] I used headings, bullets and short paragraphs.
- [ ] I gave a concrete takeaway.
- [ ] I ended with a next step.
- [ ] The post is genuinely useful and specific.
