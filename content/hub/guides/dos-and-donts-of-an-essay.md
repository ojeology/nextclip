An essay's job is to make one point clearly and support it. These dos and don'ts cover the most common things that make an essay work — or fall apart.

## Do

- **Do state a clear thesis.** End your introduction with one sentence that says what you argue and, ideally, the reasons that support it.
- **Do make each paragraph one point.** A body paragraph should make exactly one argument. If it makes two, split them.
- **Do support every claim.** For each point, give a reason, an example, or evidence. An unsupported claim is just an opinion.
- **Do use a clear order.** Put your points in a logical sequence so the reader follows the argument from start to end.
- **Do address the counter-argument.** Acknowledge the other side and answer it. This makes your argument stronger and more honest.
- **Do restate the thesis in the conclusion.** Return to your point and show why it matters, without introducing new arguments.

## Don't

- **Don't be vague.** A thesis like "essays are important" says nothing. Be specific about what you argue and why.
- **Don't mix points in one paragraph.** This is the most common essay weakness — it makes the reader work to follow you.
- **Don't pad.** A paragraph that does not support your point, however well written, should be cut. Length is not the goal; argument is.
- **Don't skip the counter-argument.** Ignoring the other side makes your essay feel one-sided and less persuasive.
- **Don't introduce new ideas in the conclusion.** The conclusion is for closing, not for starting a new thread.
- **Don't write as you speak, message-style.** Use full, clear sentences and a serious-but-plain tone.

## The quick test

Read your introduction aloud. If someone could state your thesis back to you in one line, you have a strong essay. If they cannot, tighten it.

## Checklist

- [ ] My introduction ends with a specific thesis.
- [ ] Each body paragraph makes one point.
- [ ] Every claim is supported.
- [ ] My argument is in a clear order.
- [ ] I addressed the counter-argument.
- [ ] My conclusion closes without new points.
