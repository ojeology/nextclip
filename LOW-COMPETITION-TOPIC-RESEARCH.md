# Low-Competition / High-Demand Topic Research
**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Scope:** thebryme.com content desks (Money · Sports · Fitness · Entertainment, notes on Tech)
**Brief:** topics with real, durable search demand where Google's page 1–2 is weak — the queries where people actually **scroll to page 3 or 4** because nothing answers the *question* cleanly.

---

## What "page 3 or 4" means (selection criteria)

A topic qualifies when **most** of these are true:

1. **Question-shaped query** ("why…", "what does… mean", "how does… actually work") — not a head term ("insurance", "tennis").
2. **SERP dominated by the wrong page type**: vendor blogs, forum threads, PDFs, or one official body page nobody can read (USGA, ECB rulebooks). No clean explainer owns it.
3. **Stable or rising demand** — either evergreen confusion (APR vs APY) or a fast-growing activity (Hyrox, padel) whose explainer layer hasn't been written yet.
4. **We can win with format, not authority** — one page that finally explains it plainly, internally linked to our existing cluster.
5. **AdSense-safe** (money = saving-oriented evergreen, no speculative trading; sports = no betting; fitness = general info, red flags → professional).

**Evidence tags:** `[V]` verified with live sources this session · `[J]` judgment from query-pattern knowledge — validate in GSC before writing.

## Demand-verification playbook (free, before each batch)

1. **GSC**: check impressions on adjacent existing pages (e.g. `rpe-and-rir-explained` impressions predict `what-does-3x10-mean-explained`).
2. **Google autocomplete + People Also Ask** in incognito, tier-1 locales (US/UK/CA/AU) — question persistence = demand.
3. **`site:reddit.com <query>`** — if forums dominate page 1, the SERP is beatable.
4. **Competitor scan**: if page 1 is vendor blogs / PDFs / listicles → green light; if it's Investopedia/NHS/official body with a good page → deprioritise.
5. Re-check every `[J]` tag with 1–3 of the above before writing; `[V]` items (Hyrox, padel/pickleball, soap opera effect) are verified 2026-09-26.
