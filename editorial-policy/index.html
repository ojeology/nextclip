Editorial Policy

How NEXTCLIP writes and publishes its articles.

Purpose

NEXTCLIP articles are written for entertainment and discovery purposes: to help readers find something worth watching, understand why a title works, and compare options. They are not academic reviews and not objective buyer-style rankings unless explicitly stated.

Opinions are subjective

Many articles are opinionated. Authors share personal views — which series gripped them, which anime they prefer and why. These opinions belong to the author and are presented as opinions, not universal facts.

Facts and sources

Where articles state factual information — release years, episode counts, plot basics — NEXTCLIP aims for accuracy and checks facts against reliable sources. External sources may be referenced where appropriate. If a specific fact cannot be verified, it is not invented.

Distinguishing opinion from fact

NEXTCLIP tries to keep the distinction clear: descriptive information (what a series is about, when it aired) is presented as information, while judgments (whether it is good, which one is better) are presented as the author's opinion.

Corrections

When errors are discovered — in facts, links or metadata — NEXTCLIP will correct them as soon as practical. Readers are encouraged to report errors via the Contact page.

Sponsored content and advertising

NEXTCLIP does not currently publish sponsored content or advertising. If sponsored or paid content is introduced in the future, it will be clearly identified as such so readers always know what they are reading.
