Terms of use.

The terms for using BRYME's guides, tools and opportunity records.

Using the site

BRYME is free to read and free to use. No account is required. You may read the guides, use the tools and follow the opportunity records for your own writing, personal or commercial.

What you may do with the content

You may quote short extracts from BRYME's guides with attribution and a link. You may use anything the tools produce from your own text however you like — that output is yours, not BRYME's.

You may not republish BRYME guides wholesale, or scrape the opportunity records to build a competing listing service.

Opportunity records are information, not offers

A publication record on BRYME is a summary of another organisation's public submission guideline. It is not a job offer, not a contract, and not an invitation from BRYME.

BRYME has no authority over any publication's decisions and cannot submit, advocate or intervene on your behalf. Your relationship is with the publication.

Accuracy and change

BRYME checks records against official guidelines and dates every check, but guidelines change without notice. Always confirm on the publication's own page before you pitch. See the disclaimer.

Third-party links

BRYME links to publications' own sites. BRYME does not control them and is not responsible for their content, their terms or their decisions.

Contact

Questions about these terms go to the contact page.
