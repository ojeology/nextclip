1. Summary & method

BRYME is building a database of real, currently advertised job opportunities in Lagos, Nigeria. At this stage the work is research, not verification. For each lead we record: the original source, exact title, employer, location, posting date, deadline where stated, a legitimate public employer/recruiter contact where one is publicly available, and the original application URL. A contact is a company channel, not proof a vacancy is open. We never invent an email, phone, salary, deadline, employer, status or application method; where no legitimate contact exists we say so and point to the original listing.

Scope note: This report covers Batch 1 (the 20 supplied starter leads, re-checked against their original sources) and Batch 3 (category-expansion leads). Leads formerly flagged as unconfirmable are marked; location mismatches and expired vacancies are preserved as evidence, never presented as open Lagos jobs.

9. Data integrity & caution notes

  • Never invented any email, phone, salary, deadline, employer, status or application method. Contacts are from official employer pages/LinkedIn or published board listings.
  • Do not conflate similarly named companies (US "Simply HR Inc" ≠ Lekki "Simply HRM").
  • Directory phones (BlueCoral) are MEDIUM/LOW — re-confirm identity first.
  • Daspris asks for a passport photo at application — advise seekers not to send sensitive ID at this stage.
  • Remote gigs (Cashir, Brint) — verify via listing only; beware any request to pay to "join."
  • 🟡 Research Lead ≠ verified. 🔴 closed records are evidence, not published openings. AdSense remains disabled.
