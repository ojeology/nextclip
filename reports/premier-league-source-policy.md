# BRYME Sports — Premier League source and verification policy

Last reviewed: 14 August 2026

## Source order
1. **Premier League official website and official app** — fixtures, fixture changes, tables, results, match reports and competition announcements.
2. **Official club websites and club communications** — squad announcements, manager statements, confirmed player availability and stadium information.
3. **UEFA, FA and EFL official channels** — competition, disciplinary and promotion/relegation context.
4. **Named reputable reporting** — BBC Sport, Reuters, Sky Sports, The Athletic and ESPN may be used only when the original source is identified or when a fact is independently corroborated.

## Matchweek 1 fixture verification
The 2026/27 opening round kickoff times and broadcast listings were checked against:
- Premier League, *Fixture Schedule Released for Season 2026/27* (19 June 2026)
- Premier League, *All 380 fixtures for 2026/27 Premier League season* (19 June 2026)
- Premier League, *All of Arsenal's fixtures for 2026/27 Premier League season* (19 June 2026)

Fixtures remain subject to broadcast, policing and cup-related amendments. Match pages must be rechecked against the official Premier League fixture listing before matchday.

## Editorial rule
Do not publish a transfer, injury, suspension, expected lineup, quote, statistic or result without a dated, attributable source. Where information is provisional, label it as such and give a last-checked date. Unresolved source conflicts stay in Review.
