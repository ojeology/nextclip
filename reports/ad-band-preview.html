What to notice
PositionAfter </main>, before <footer> — the very bottom. Nothing above it moves.
LabelledA visible "Advertisement" tag. Required so it can never be mistaken for your editorial content.
Reserved height280px blocked out in advance, so there is no layout shift (CLS) when the ad loads.
Responsivedata-full-width-responsive="true" — adapts to screen width instead of overflowing.
CSP-safePushes via an external /assets/ads-init.js. An inline push would be blocked by your own security policy.
No pop-upsStatic, in-flow, below the fold. It keeps the "no pop-ups, no interstitials" promise on your homepage.
