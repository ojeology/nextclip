# MASTER TEMPLATE — verification enquiry (copy for any lead)

Fill the `[square brackets]`. Keep it short and honest. One enquiry per email/WhatsApp.

---

## EMAIL

**To:** `[recipient@company.com]`
**Subject:** `[Job Title] — is this still open? [Date]`

> Dear `[Team name / Recruiter]`,
>
> I am `[Your name]` and I run BRYME, an independent job/opportunity information platform in Nigeria.
>
> We came across your advertised **`[Job Title]`** at **`[Location]`** and wanted to confirm it is still open and accepting applications as of **`[Today's date]`**. Could you also confirm the correct way to apply?
>
> To be clear, BRYME is not affiliated with your company — we only verify that the publicly advertised vacancy is current so job seekers are not misled.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> **`[Your name]`** · BRYME (independent job information platform)
> Contact: `[your email / phone]`
