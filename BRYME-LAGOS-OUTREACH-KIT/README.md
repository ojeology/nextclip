## How to use
1. Pick the employer file for the lead you're verifying (Priority-1 employers first).
2. **Fill the placeholders** in [square brackets] — your name, the exact role, the date.
3. For **email**: paste into your mail client, set the subject line exactly as shown, attach nothing.
4. For **WhatsApp**: find the published WhatsApp/number, send the message; keep it to one polite paragraph (short = higher reply rate).
5. **Log every reply** in `BRYME-LAGOS-VERIFICATION-BATCH2.md` → Section 3 (contact log). Never record a status you didn't actually receive.

> ✅ Golden rules from the pipeline: don't invent a contact; don't claim you represent the company; don't log "verified" unless the employer or an official channel confirms it. Every number and address here is from an official employer page or a published board listing.
