AI-likeness checker.

A rule-based heuristic that flags formulaic phrases and AI-favoured words. Honest by design: it nudges you to sound more natural, it does not pretend to detect AI.

← All tools

A rule-based heuristic look for formulaic phrases and AI-favoured words in your text. It is an honest nudge to sound more natural — not a real AI detector (no tool can reliably do that).

About this tool

How to use the ai-likeness checker.

What it does. A rule-based heuristic that flags formulaic phrases and AI-favoured wording in your text - an honesty nudge, not a verdict.

How to use it. Paste the text, analyse, and judge each flag. Where a flagged phrase does real work, keep it; where it is filler - in today's fast-paced world and friends - rewrite it in your own voice. No tool can reliably detect AI; this one detects the style.

Why it matters. If you edit AI-drafted text, the risk is shipping generic prose under your name. This finds the generic so your voice can replace it.

Your data. The tool runs entirely in your browser - text you paste never leaves the page, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Good to know

Treat every flag as a question, not an instruction - the goal is a draft that sounds like you, not a draft that avoids a word list. If a flagged phrase is genuinely yours, keep it without guilt.

Where this fits

Quality tools are the last pass, not the first: they catch what familiarity has made you blind to once the real work is done. Used at the end they are a safety net; used at the start they become a way to avoid writing. Finish the draft first, then let the checks be ruthless on your behalf. The order matters less than the habit: same pass, same checks, every time, so nothing depends on how tired you are.

What The Tool Measures

The AI writing checker measures style patterns associated with machine text: burstiness, perplexity and repetition shape the score, and the honest limits are stated plainly. The tool is a style lens, not a verdict.

  • What it measures Burstiness, perplexity and repetition patterns.
  • The honest limits No detector proves authorship; the score is a signal.
  • False positives Formulaic human writing trips detectors daily.
  • Style shifts What the scores say about the writing itself.
  • Ethical use The tool for revision, never for accusation.
  • Reading the score How to use the number without worshipping it.

An AI-likeness score is a style measurement with real false positives. Use it to revise prose and never to prosecute writers. Using The Score Honestly

The checker's score is a revision signal: high AI-likeness flags formulaic stretches worth rewriting, and the honest workflow uses it as a style mirror rather than a courtroom exhibit.

  • Revision signal High scores mark stretches where the prose went flat.
  • Rewrite, do not delete Flagged passages usually need a human voice, not removal.
  • Never prosecute Accusation use fails the tool's own false-positive rate.
  • Compare drafts Score movement between drafts shows style work landing.
  • Formulaic traps Templates and lists trip scores by design; know yours.
  • The writer's voice Specific detail and surprise are what detectors miss anyway.

An AI-likeness score belongs in the revision toolkit and nowhere else. Use it to sharpen style, keep the human voice loud, and let the writing carry its own proof.
