How this calendar stays honest

Rows appear only where a record carries its own verified deadline data — 34 of the 142 publications do. The rest are rolling or unstated and live in the full database rather than here. Spot a moved date? Tell us and the record gets re-verified. For the market-wide picture, see the State of Paid Writing 2026 report.

Windows, with their sources. Submission windows are recorded from each publication's guideline with a check date, and the desk cross-references the wider market through Poets & Writers' magazine database. Where a window is stated as rolling rather than dated, the entry says so — an undated window is a different kind of deadline.
