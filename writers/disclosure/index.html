Transparency

How BRYME makes money.

The short version: it does not yet — and when it does, the ways are listed here first.

Current status

Right now: no ads, no affiliate links, no paid placement.

  • Opportunity listings cannot be bought. Publications get on this site by being verified, and stay on it the same way. Nobody pays to be listed, ranked, or kept.
  • No affiliate links exist on the site today. When that changes, every affected guide carries a visible note at the top, and this page names the programmes.
  • Advertising is planned, not present — it follows the editorial policy: content first, never designed around ads, ads never dressed as job cards or application buttons.

When links are ever monetised

The rules that will apply.

  • Recommendations are criteria-first. Each software guide states what it compares on; commissions do not enter the criteria, and free tools are recommended over paid ones when they serve the writer better.
  • Marked in context. Money links are identifiable as such in the sentence, not buried in a footnote.
  • The verification record stays out. Opportunity pages, status labels and rate data are never monetised and never influenced by any commercial relationship.
  • This page is updated before any programme goes live, not after.

Questions about any of this — or a report that a link looks like an exception — go straight to the contact page and into the public corrections log when warranted.
