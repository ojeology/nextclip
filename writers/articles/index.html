How this section works

Editorial standards, in plain language.

People are asked, not assumed

Every story is reported from conversations with the people in it. Quotes are checked back with the person who said them before publication.

Dates mean something

A story carries the date it was published and, where it was revised, the date it changed. Nothing is quietly rewritten and left looking new.

Corrections are public

If something here turns out to be wrong, it is corrected on the page and recorded in the corrections log.

No invented numbers

Reading times are computed from the text. Nothing on this page ranks stories by popularity unless that ranking comes from measured reads.

The full detail is in the editorial policy. Reviewed 23 November 2026.
