Rights

Copyright and source use.

Who owns what on BRYME, and how BRYME treats other people's material.

BRYME's own work

The guides, tools, opportunity summaries and site design are BRYME's original work. Short quotations with attribution and a link are welcome. Wholesale republication is not.

How BRYME treats publications' material

An opportunity record is BRYME's own summary of a publicly available submission guideline — written in BRYME's words, not copied from the publication. Each record names and links to the original source.

BRYME does not reproduce guidelines in full, does not copy publications' page designs, and does not present another organisation's opportunity as its own.

Names and trademarks

Publication names and trademarks belong to their owners. They appear here for identification, so a writer knows which publication a record describes. Their presence does not imply endorsement, affiliation or a partnership.

Quoting other people's writing

BRYME's craft guides discuss technique using original examples. Where a real text is discussed, it is quoted only as briefly as the point requires and is attributed. BRYME does not reproduce song lyrics, poems or substantial extracts of copyrighted work.

Rights concerns

If you believe something here uses your material improperly, contact the desk with the page URL and what you would like changed. Genuine rights concerns are acted on quickly rather than argued about.

Using BRYME's work

Quote freely with a visible link to the page you are quoting. Republishing a whole page, or framing BRYME's summaries as your own research, is not covered by that welcome — and re-checking a record yourself is always better than copying one, because the verification date on the original is the whole point of it.

Copyright And Permissions

The copyright position

The content on this site — the writing, the tables, the tools and their code, the design — is the work of the desk and its writers and is protected by copyright as it is published. All rights are reserved except where a page explicitly states otherwise. The copyright line on each page reflects the publication date of that page; the date matters because the content is maintained and dated material supersedes earlier versions.

Quoting and sharing

Readers may quote short extracts for discussion, review and reference with clear attribution and a link to the source page. This is how the site is meant to be used: a table cited in an argument, a method described with a link back, a figure referenced with the date it carried. Commercial republication, repackaging substantial sections as another publication’s work, and dataset extraction for resale require written permission.

Third-party material

Where the site references third-party material — names, product marks, quoted rules — it does so for identification, review and explanation under fair dealing. The marks belong to their owners and no affiliation or endorsement is implied by their appearance. External sources are linked rather than reproduced so the attribution sits with the reproduction; where a source requires removal of a quote, the desk removes it.

How to request permission

Permission requests reach the desk through the contact route on this site and are answered with a real reply: what is being used, where, for how long and on what terms. Educational and non-profit uses are handled generously. The desk also acts on notices: genuinely infringing material is removed promptly on a proper notice, and the desk complies with the applicable takedown process in good faith.

This desk specifically

On this site, the writing desk covers craft, workflow and the tools working writers use, with guides written from newsroom and freelance practice rather than content-marketing theory and every tool page stating its own honest limits. The pages on this desk are written by the same editorial process that governs the whole site: sourced claims, dated figures and dated verdicts, applied here to writers-desk subject matter.

The desk-specific pages on this site — the guides, reviews and tools under the writers section — each carry their own method notes and review dates. Where a policy on this page and a practice on an individual page disagree, the individual page’s dated note is the specific record and this page is the general one.

The working list

  • Content protected as published; all rights reserved
  • Short attributed extracts and links welcome
  • Commercial republication needs written permission
  • Third-party marks used for identification only
  • Permission requests answered with real terms
  • Genuine notices actioned promptly and in good faith

Background context for this page is cross-checked against the encyclopaedic record where a reference helps. The desk’s standard applies across the archive: named entities carry sources, measured figures carry dates and claims the desk cannot verify are marked rather than rounded. Policies are only as good as their practice, and the practice on this site is public: the pages carry their dates, the corrections carry their notes and the review cycle is a calendar rather than a mood. A reader who finds the practice and the policy disagreeing should write to the desk through the contact page — that message is the most useful one the desk receives, and it is answered.
