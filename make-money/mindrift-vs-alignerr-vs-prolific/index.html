Mindrift vs Alignerr vs Prolific: Which Pays Nigerians More?

All three accept Nigerians, all three pay in dollars, and all three are free to join. The real difference is what kind of contributor each one rewards.

Mindrift — the steady queue

Mindrift’s AI-training tasks suit contributors who want a queue that moves. General work realistically lands in the $8–25 per hour band; higher-paying expert boards require verifiable domain expertise. Payouts run bi-weekly through Payoneer, which Nigerian contributors consistently rate above PayPal for reliability.

Alignerr — the specialist ceiling

Alignerr pays for expert alignment work, and the ceiling is the highest of the three: specialists list up to $150 per hour. The catch is the entry bar — you need real domain expertise or genuinely strong writing or coding skill, plus ID verification. If you qualify for a specialist lane, Alignerr usually out-earns the other two.

Prolific — the low-commitment lane

Prolific is different: it pays per completed research study, typically $8–20, matched to your demographic profile. Availability swings with what researchers need — a Nigerian profile may see fewer studies than a US one — but each study is short, ethical-academic in nature, and paid promptly via PayPal. It is the best platform to run alongside the other two.

So which one should a Nigerian pick?

  • You have a professional domain (law, medicine, engineering, code): Alignerr first, Mindrift’s expert boards second.
  • You have strong English and consistency: Mindrift general queue first.
  • You want something alongside either: Prolific, always.

All three are free to join, verified on the BRYME platform reviews table, and honest about queues being unpredictable. Start from the Nigeria guide, register on two, and let the queues decide your week.

Frequently asked questions

Which pays more in Nigeria: Mindrift, Alignerr or Prolific?

For specialists, Alignerr has the highest listed ceiling (up to $150/hr). For steady general work, Mindrift's realistic $8–25/hr band is the benchmark. Prolific pays per study (typically $8–20) and depends on demographic demand.

Do all three pay out to Nigeria?

Yes, with different rails: Mindrift pays via Payoneer bi-weekly, Alignerr via PayPal or bank transfer, and Prolific via PayPal. Payoneer and Wise are usually the smoothest for Nigerian payouts.

Can I use all three at the same time?

Yes — running two or three platforms is the standard advice, because task queues empty unpredictably on every single one of them.
