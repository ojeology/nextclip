Pocket Option, Expert Option, Quotex: it’s a trap

Promoters don’t pick those names by accident. They get paid when you sign up and deposit.

Open Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp in Nigeria and you will see the same three apps: Pocket Option, Expert Option, Quotex.

Someone in a rented car. A screenshot of “today’s profit.” A WhatsApp “signals” group. “Start with ₦10,000.” “I will teach you.” Always their link.

That is the product. Not trading. Getting you to deposit through an affiliate link.

Why those three?

Those platforms pay promoters a cut when you register and fund an account. That is why the same names appear in every video. If it was about finding you the safest place to put money, they would not all be shouting the same three apps.

Ask a simple question: if this was easy money, why does the person need your deposit?

This is not a job

You are not getting paid to work. You are betting that a price will be “up” or “down” in 30 seconds, one minute, five minutes. That is a short-expiry bet dressed up as trading.

The payout looks generous — 70%, 80%, 90% — until you count the losses. Win some, lose some, and the house still has the edge. That is how the app stays in business.

Most people who try this lose money. The ones who post wins are either lucky this week, or they are selling a course.

The usual script

  • Fake or old profit screenshots.
  • “VIP signals” that cost extra, after you already deposited.
  • Pressure to deposit more after the first loss (“recover”).
  • A link that pays the promoter, not you.
  • No talk of how you get money out, only how you put it in.

Licensing, in plain words

These apps are not licensed by Nigeria’s SEC. They are offshore. If something goes wrong — withdrawal delayed, account frozen, “bonus” you cannot cash — you have almost no local protection.

Accessible is not the same as safe. “Not banned” is not the same as “good for you.”

What to do instead

If someone needs you to use their Pocket Option, Expert Option or Quotex link, walk away. That is not a mentor. That is a salesman.

Real online money is slower and boring: writing that pays, remote work, coding. Those are on BRYME. We do not take a cut of your deposit.

This is not financial advice. It is a warning. Do not send money to an app because a stranger on the internet said “trust me.”
