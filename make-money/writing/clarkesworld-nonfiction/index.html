Clarkesworld Magazine

Non-fiction articles for SF/F readers

Clarkesworld pays 10 US cents a word, up to 2,500 words, for original articles of interest to science-fiction and fantasy readers. Query first — do not send a finished article. AI-assisted articles are not being considered.

What they want

  • Articles on the genre publishing business, the writing or reading process, or scientific material useful in SF stories.
  • A query letter with subject NONFICTION QUERY: [title or concept] to nonfiction@clarkesworldmagazine.com.
  • Thoughtful, in-depth, well-written subjects they have not covered.

What they don't want

  • A completed article instead of a query.
  • Reviews, literary criticism, interview pitches, reprints.
  • Articles written, co-written or assisted by AI.
  • Convention reports, award rants, ten-rules lists, personal alien-abduction memoirs.

Requirements

  • Query first
  • Original to Clarkesworld
  • No AI

Rights

Not separately restated for non-fiction beyond originality. See the official page.

How to submit

  • Read the non-fiction section of https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/submissions/.
  • Email nonfiction@clarkesworldmagazine.com.
