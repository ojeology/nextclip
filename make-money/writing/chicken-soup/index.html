Chicken Soup for the Soul

True first-person stories and story-poems

Chicken Soup for the Soul pays $250 plus 10 free copies if they publish your true first-person story in a book — not for submitting. Official: writers from anywhere, English. AI and AI-assisted work (including Grammarly) are banned. Current official book topics have deadlines in September–November 2026.

Current official topics (check the page — they change): Count your blessings 15 Sept 2026; Random acts of kindness 30 Sept 2026; Humorous stories 15 Oct 2026; Married/couples 30 Nov 2026Last verified 2026-08-19

What they want

  • A true first-person story with a beginning, middle and end, starting in the action.
  • Heartwarming, funny or extraordinary ordinary-life moments. Not a sermon, essay, eulogy, term paper, politics piece, biography or newspaper-style article about a stranger.
  • Poems only if they tell a story.
  • Submit only to a listed official book topic.

What they don't want

  • AI-generated or AI-assisted work. Official page names Grammarly and says do not use AI at all.
  • Fiction. Previously published work except a small local publication or your own blog.
  • Book manuscripts from the public. Title ideas from the public.
  • Stories that do not match a listed book topic.

Requirements

  • Website form only: https://www.chickensoup.com/story-submissions/submit-your-story
  • True story, first person, usually ≤1,200 words
  • Match a current official book topic

Rights

If selected you sign their standard permission release. Broader rights language is not copied here because the rest of that agreement is not fully quoted on the public guidelines page.

How to submit

  • Pick a topic on https://www.chickensoup.com/story-submissions/possible-book-topics — only use deadlines on that page.
  • Submit via the official form. Success screen is the only confirmation.
  • Simultaneous submissions are allowed if you say so in the comments or email webmaster@chickensoupforthesoul.com.
