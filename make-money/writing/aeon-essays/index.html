What they want

  • When open: a pitch, not a completed draft. Perennial (not news-tied), cosmopolitan, pluralist.
  • Expect 3–5 drafts, fact-check and copy-edit. About three months from a good first draft to publication.

What they don't want

  • Do not pitch while the official page says closed.
  • Completed drafts. Rebuttals. News commentary. Book reviews.
  • Drafts wholly or largely written by AI. Do not upload editor emails or contracted drafts to an LLM.

Requirements

  • Wait until the official pitch page reopens
  • Pitch, not a full draft

Rights

Author retains copyright. Aeon gets first publication, exclusive digital rights for five years and exclusive print for one year, then non-exclusive. Ideas pieces may be CC BY-ND.

How to submit

  • Do not use the form while it says closed.
  • Watch https://aeon.co/pitch.
