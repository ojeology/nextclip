Listverse

Top-10 fact lists

Listverse pays $100 USD (minus PayPal fees) for accepted 10-item lists of 1,500–2,000 words. Official submit page: only US, UK, NZ, Ireland, Canada, Australia and South Africa, unless you are already a published Listverse writer. Nigeria is not on that list.

What they want

  • A finished 10-item list with an introductory paragraph, 1,500–2,000 words, native-level English.
  • Quality online sources for each item. No Wikipedia or other editable pages. Format sources as in section 7 of the Author’s Guide.
  • Popular official themes: mysteries, secrets, facts/misconceptions, history with a twist, the darker side of people. Uniqueness is required — do not rework a list already on Listverse or elsewhere.
  • American spelling. Imperial measures with metric in parentheses except in scientific pieces.

What they don't want

  • Writers outside US, UK, NZ, Ireland, Canada, Australia and South Africa (unless already published there).
  • Sports, technology, or self-help / advice (official guide: almost never published).
  • Personal or unverifiable material. Translations of existing foreign-language pages. Content farms / middle-men.
  • Images (their editors add images). Illegal content. Plagiarism.
  • Sending two or more lists in one day — they treat that as a sign you rushed.

Requirements

  • Live in one of the seven named countries, or already be a published Listverse writer
  • PayPal
  • Finished 10-item list
  • Sources for every item

Rights

On purchase: ownership, copyright and all other rights including moral rights transfer to Listverse Limited. They may edit. If rejected, rights stay with you.

How to submit

  • Read https://listverse.com/authors/listverse-author-guide.pdf first.
  • Submit through https://listverse.com/submit-a-list/ and agree to the terms.
  • If you have not heard in 30 days, check spam. Official comms come from submit@listverse.com.
  • They do not negotiate rejected lists.
