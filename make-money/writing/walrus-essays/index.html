The Walrus

Essays and reported features

The Walrus official submissions page: short essays of around 1,000 words start at $500; long-form reported features pay up to $1 per word. Pitch features and essays to pitch@thewalrus.ca. Fiction is Canadian writers only and is closed until January.

What they want

  • A pitch with the story, original research, approach, format, length, and why you. Samples as links or attachments.
  • Essays: focused argument, original and significant, deeper than a newspaper op-ed.
  • Features: stories not subjects — characters, access, narrative.
  • Memoir: completed draft only.
  • Note if it is a simultaneous submission.

What they don't want

  • A story The Walrus already covered.
  • Fiction or poetry in this inbox. Fiction is Canadian writers, windows 1 Jan–28 Feb and 1 Jun–31 Jul only — closed on 19 August 2026.

Requirements

  • Email pitch@thewalrus.ca
  • Description, research, length, clips

Rights

Writers retain derivative rights. The page does not spell out first serial rights in more detail.

How to submit

  • Read https://thewalrus.ca/about/submissions/.
  • Email pitch@thewalrus.ca for essays and features.
  • Fiction: fiction@thewalrus.ca only in the stated windows. Poetry: poetry@thewalrus.ca.
