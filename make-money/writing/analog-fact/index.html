Analog Science Fiction and Fact

Science fact articles

Analog pays 9 US cents a word for fact articles of about 4,000 words on present research frontiers whose likely future developments have wide interest. Same official guidelines page as Analog fiction. AI-assisted work is banned.

OpenLast verified 2026-08-19

Official information

Overview

Publication
Analog Science Fiction and Fact
Eligibility
Official: any writer, solely on merit. No country list. Readers are technically knowledgeable but from many backgrounds — specialised jargon should be kept to a necessary minimum.
Writing
Science fact article
Payment
9¢ per word
Currency
USD
Payment conditions
Official fact-article rate only — not the fiction rate. About 4,000 words. Necessary images must be provided by the author in camera-ready form. Payment is on acceptance.

On acceptance
Word count
About 4,000 words
Response time
Average two to three months; query after four months

Open

Not publicly stated
How to submit
Official online submissions system — not email
AI policy
AI-generated work prohibited

What they want

  • Subjects of current and future interest — present research frontiers whose likely future developments have implications of wide interest.
  • A more informal style than a professional journal. Analog readers read for entertainment as well as information.

What they don't want

  • Specialised jargon and mathematical detail beyond a necessary minimum.
  • AI-written, developed or assisted work.

Requirements

  • About 4,000 words
  • Camera-ready images if needed
  • No AI

Rights

Same official Must Read Magazines rights framework described on the Analog guidelines page.

How to submit

  • Read the Fact Articles section at https://analogsf.com/contact-us/writers-guidelines/.
  • Submit through https://analog.magazinesubmissions.com/.
