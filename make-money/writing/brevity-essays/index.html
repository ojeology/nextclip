Brevity

Flash nonfiction (750 words or fewer)

Brevity pays a $45 honorarium via PayPal for featured flash essays of 750 words or fewer. Official Submittable: the Brief Nonfiction category is open September–April. In August it is closed. AI-generated or AI-assisted work is rejected. Writers from many countries have been published; there is no country list.
