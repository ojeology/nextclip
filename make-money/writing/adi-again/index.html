Again column

What they want

  • A love letter to a treasured text you return to again and again — political texts, poetry, novels or creative nonfiction.
  • Pitch: the title of the book plus a short summary (2 paragraphs maximum) of why you chose it.
  • Include 1–2 links to previous work.
  • Subject line: AGAIN PITCH.
  • Adi describes itself as a literary magazine of global politics dedicated to rehumanizing policy.

What they don't want

  • Attachments unless editors request them.
  • Submissions or pitches generated with AI.
  • Work previously published in English.
  • Hate speech or discriminatory language.
  • More than one Adi submission at a time — wait until the current one is accepted or declined.

Requirements

  • Email admin@adimagazine.com
  • Subject: AGAIN PITCH
  • Book title + max 2-paragraph summary
  • 1–2 links to previous work
  • No attachment unless requested

Rights

Adi acquires first exclusive, world English-language publication rights. Copyright remains with the author.

How to submit

  • Email admin@adimagazine.com with subject AGAIN PITCH.
  • Do not send an attachment unless an editor asks.
  • Simultaneous submissions are allowed; withdraw if accepted elsewhere.
