Climate Home News

Climate news, features and investigations

Climate Home News commissions freelance reporting from around the world. Official: rates start at £0.35 a word (GBP). Pitch under 300 words — not a finished article. Comment pieces on the same page are unpaid. Do not use AI to write pitches or stories.

What they want

  • Climate stories with an international outlook: finance, carbon markets, energy transition, critical minerals, human rights, land conflict, loss and damage, greenwash, climate diplomacy.
  • What is new, why it matters internationally, main actors, sources, your background, where the action is.
  • News/analysis/features: Megan Rowling (mr at climatehomenews.com). Investigations: Sebastian Rodriguez (sr at climatehomenews.com).

What they don't want

  • A finished article instead of a pitch.
  • Stories based only on a research paper, a widely distributed report, or a conference panel.
  • Stories solely about climate impacts or a wind farm unless there is something new.
  • AI-written pitches. Official: they will not respond to pitches that appear AI-generated.
  • Comment if you expect pay — official: comment is generally unpaid.

Requirements

  • Short pitch, not a draft
  • Climate + international angle
  • No AI pitch
