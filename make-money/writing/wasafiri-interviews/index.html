Wasafiri

Interviews

Wasafiri is open year-round for interviews via its Taylor & Francis portal. Official typical fee is £150 for a transcribed and edited interview of 4,000–5,000 words. Fiction, life writing and poetry are closed on the same page. AI-generated content is not permitted.

  • A transcribed and edited interview of 4,000–5,000 words submitted through the official portal.

What they don't want

  • Fiction, life writing or poetry while those doors are closed.
