Personal Essay

The Republic pays ₦100,000 for Personal Essays of 2,001–3,000 words. Submit through the official account form. Other categories have their own rates on the official submissions page.

This figure is the official Personal Essay fee only. The Republic states that rates vary by format, length and editorial demands. What they want

  • Personal essays that combine strong writing with clear insight, reflection and relevance to broader questions shaping Nigerian and African life.
  • The magazine publishes journalism, essays, criticism and fiction grounded in intellectual seriousness, strong reporting and clarity of thought.

What they don't want

  • AI policy is not stated on the official submissions page.
  • Do not treat the Personal Essay rate as the rate for every Republic category.

Requirements

  • Sign in or create an account on The Republic
  • Use Submissions and complete the official form
  • Pitch through that form — the FAQ does not invite email pitches

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official submissions page.

How to submit

  • Go to https://rpublc.com/submissions
  • Log in or create an account.
  • Open Submissions and complete the form. Editors contact you if the piece is a fit.
