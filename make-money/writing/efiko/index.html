Efiko Magazine

8th edition — poetry, short story, essay

Efiko is accepting poetry, short stories and essays for its 8th edition until 30 August 2026. Nigerian-account contributors are paid ₦20,000 or ₦30,000 depending on length. Work sent outside the window is deleted unread.

Payment conditions
For contributors with a Nigerian bank account: ₦30,000 for 2 or more poems, or for prose over 2,500 published words; ₦20,000 for accepted submissions below those thresholds. Paid upon publication. Contributors with non-Nigerian bank accounts currently receive a novel or nonfiction book by a selected African author if Amazon delivers to their country, value capped at $35 including processing — not a cash fee. Do not describe Efiko as paying every contributor ₦30,000.

What they want

  • Poems, short stories and essays of high quality from writers of African origin anywhere, or from people writing about Africa.
  • Times New Roman, 12pt, double-spaced, numbered pages.
  • One piece per issue.
  • A 5-line bio in the email body.

What they don't want

  • Work sent outside the submission window is deleted unread.
  • Except in special cases, only original, previously unpublished work. Work that already exists online is not accepted.
  • More than one piece per issue.
  • Submissions that do not name the genre in the email subject line are not read.
  • Extensive revision after acceptance is not allowed. Title decisions are final.

Requirements

  • Word document attachment
  • Subject line states the genre
  • Filename is your name + genre
  • 5-line bio in the email
  • Times New Roman 12pt, double-spaced, numbered pages

Rights

First serial rights; rights to republish in a collection; shared rights for maiden adaptations. Text rights revert to the writer 90 days after publication. If you reprint or repurpose the work, you must acknowledge Efiko as first publisher.

How to submit

  • Email a Word document to submissions@efikomag.com.
  • Put the genre in the subject line.
  • Name the file with your name and the genre.
  • Include a 5-line bio in the email body.
  • Simultaneous submissions are allowed; notify Efiko promptly if accepted elsewhere.
  • If you have no response after 3 months, the official page says to inquire at editors@efikomag.com.
