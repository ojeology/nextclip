The New Humanitarian

Humanitarian news, analysis, first person, opinion

The New Humanitarian’s official pitch page pays 40 cents per published word ($40 per image). First-person essays are 1,000–1,200 words. Do not use AI to write pitches. Africa pitches go to the Africa editors listed on that page.

What they want

  • Stories from conflicts and disasters that improve understanding of people in humanitarian crises.
  • Official formats include news, news features, analysis, 800-word opinion, 1,000–1,200-word first-person essays, investigations, photo features, interviews, podcasts.
  • A pitch with working headline, why it matters, unique angle, who you will interview, and a risk assessment if needed.
  • Africa: Philip Kleinfeld (philip@thenewhumanitarian.org) or Obi Anyadike (obinna@thenewhumanitarian.org).

What they don't want

  • AI-written pitches. Official: hear from you, not AI.
  • Stories other outlets are already covering, unless you add a distinct humanitarian angle.

Requirements

  • Email the relevant editor listed on the official pitch page
  • No AI pitch
  • Headline, angle, sources, risk note if needed

Rights

Not publicly stated on the pitch page.

How to submit

  • Read https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/about-us/pitch-us-a-story.
  • For Africa stories email philip@thenewhumanitarian.org or obinna@thenewhumanitarian.org.
  • Investigations have a separate secure pitch page.
