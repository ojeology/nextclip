El Alboroto — Spanish-language section

El Alboroto is The Rumpus’s Spanish-language section. Official Submittable: same pay as the English magazine — $500 for prose and comics, $200 for poetry. Maximum 2,500 words. Complete work only, no pitches. AI is banned.

What they want

  • Unpublished Spanish-language work with a cover letter (max one page) and a third-person bio.
