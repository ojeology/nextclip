Overland

Online literary, political and cultural analysis

Overland’s online magazine wants short, punchy, topical literary, political and cultural analysis. Official current rate is $180 plus 12% super. Personal essays are not taken unless tightly tied to political concerns. No country restriction is stated on the nonfiction page.

Official nonfiction page and the 1 July 2026 rates note: $180 for online articles, plus 12% superannuation. What they want

  • Short, punchy, topical literary, political and cultural analysis.
  • A completed piece via Submittable, or a ~100-word pitch on the pitch page: what, why you, how it differs, when you can file.

What they don't want

  • Personal essays or creative nonfiction online unless very consistently linked to political concerns.
  • Do not invent a print-essay fee while the two official print figures disagree.

Requirements

  • Submittable or official pitch form
  • Political/cultural analysis

Rights

Copyright remains with the authors.

How to submit

  • Read https://overland.org.au/submit/nonfiction-submissions/.
  • Pitch page: https://overland.org.au/submit/pitch-to-overland/.
