NACLA

Online journalism on Latin America and the Caribbean

NACLA wants short pitches — not drafts — on Latin America, the Caribbean, US Latinx communities and US hemispheric policy. Official: a small honorarium of $100–$200 for contributors who depend on writing as their primary income. Typical online length 1,200–1,500 words. Spanish pitches are welcome.

What they want

  • A short query: the story, why you, the reporting process, why unique and timely, plus clips.
  • Journalistic narrative with sociocultural or political-economic context — more analysis than straight news.
  • Hyperlinks for verifiable facts. Pitches and drafts in Spanish are welcome.

What they don't want

  • Completed drafts or manuscripts for the web.
  • Footnotes instead of links.
  • Assuming payment if writing is not your primary income.

Requirements

  • Pitch only, to editor@nacla.org
  • Latin America / Caribbean / Latinx / US hemispheric policy

Rights

Web articles are available for republication with “Originally published in NACLA” and a link. Print magazine articles are copyrighted.

How to submit

  • Read https://nacla.org/write-for-nacla/.
  • Email editor@nacla.org. Investigations/grant ideas: jchandler@nacla.org.
