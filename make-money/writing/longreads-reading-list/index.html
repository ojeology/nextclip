What they want

  • A curated reading list: essay-style introduction, then links to longform stories on one theme that are freely available on the web.
  • Timely topics, or offbeat and unique angles. A diverse mix of publications and writers.
  • Subject line: Reading List + topic.

What they don't want

  • Reading lists that feature books exclusively. A book as a side note is occasionally fine.
  • Generative AI writing or editing.
  • Fiction.

Requirements

  • Email hello@longreads.com
  • Subject includes Reading List and the topic

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official submissions page.

How to submit

  • Email hello@longreads.com with subject Reading List + topic.
  • They generally reply only if interested. Simultaneous submissions are encouraged.
