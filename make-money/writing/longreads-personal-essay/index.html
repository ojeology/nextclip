Longreads

Personal essay

Longreads pays $500 for personal essays. They do not commission personal essays from a pitch — send a full, polished draft. Official page: writers and journalists around the world. Generative AI is not accepted.

What they want

  • A full, polished personal-essay draft. They will not commission a personal essay from a pitch.
  • Smart, unexpected angles and a compelling voice. Wide range of topics.
  • Attach PDF, Google Doc or Word, or paste the draft in the email.
  • Subject line should include the pitch type (“Personal Essay”) and the topic.
  • They do not accept fiction.

What they don't want

  • A personal-essay pitch without a finished draft.
  • Any submission or pitch written or edited using generative AI tools such as ChatGPT.
  • Fiction.

Requirements

  • Finished draft
  • Email hello@longreads.com
  • Subject includes Personal Essay and the topic

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official submissions page.

How to submit

  • Email hello@longreads.com with subject line Personal Essay + topic.
  • Attach or paste the finished draft.
  • They cannot confirm receipt. They generally reply only if interested. Simultaneous submissions are encouraged.
