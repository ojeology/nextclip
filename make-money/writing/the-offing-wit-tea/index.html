Wit Tea — humour

The Offing’s humour department, Wit Tea, is open through 1 October 2026. Official contributor fee is $25–$100 depending on department and length. No submission fee in 2026. Comedic work in any genre, no more than 8 pages. AI only if it is an intentional, disclosed critique of AI.

What they want

  • Comedic work in any genre. Strong character voices, absurdity, momentum, vulnerability, surprise.

What they don't want

  • Email submissions.
  • More than one piece per department at a time.
  • AI unless it is an intentional engagement or critique of AI, marked with where and how it was used.

Requirements

  • Submittable only
  • ≤8 pages
  • Previously unpublished in English

Rights

First serial rights worldwide in English and non-exclusive anthology rights. Contributor retains other rights and must cite The Offing if relicensed.
