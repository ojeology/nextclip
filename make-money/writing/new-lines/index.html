What they want

  • Five official sections: Reportage; Arguments (not op-eds); Anchored in History; First Person; Review.
  • A cold pitch under 300 words: envisioned lead, one or two short paragraphs, brief lit review, bio/access, proposed length and file date.
  • On spec: say so in the subject line, attach a Word document, and still include a proper pitch.
  • AP style. They generally do not use hyperlinks and never use footnotes.

What they don't want

  • Op-eds. Academic abstracts. Jargon.
  • Anything translated, written, developed or assisted by AI. Official: that can end the working relationship.
  • A made-up dollar fee.

Requirements

  • Email submissions@newlinesmag.com
  • Pitch a story, not a topic
  • No AI
