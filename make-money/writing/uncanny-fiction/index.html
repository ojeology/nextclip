Uncanny Magazine

Speculative short fiction

Uncanny’s official submissions page: original speculative fiction of 750–10,000 words pays 10 US cents a word including audio rights. The next fiction window is 24 August to 7 September 2026. AI-written work is rejected and can ban the author.

What they want

  • Original unpublished speculative fiction. Intricate, experimental stories with verve.
  • Modern manuscript format, .RTF/.DOC/.DOCX, via Moksha.
  • Cover letter: length, significant publishing history, anything relevant to that story.

What they don't want

  • Do not submit fiction before 24 August 2026.
  • Multiple stories at once. Resubmitting a rejected story.
  • Any submission written with artificial intelligence or similar technologies. Undisclosed AI can permanently bar you.

Requirements

  • Wait for 24 August 2026
  • Moksha only
  • 750–10,000 words
  • No AI

Rights

Payment includes audio rights. Broader contract terms are not fully quoted on the public page.

How to submit

  • Watch https://www.uncannymagazine.com/submissions/.
  • When open, use https://uncannymagazine.moksha.io/publication/uncanny-magazine.
