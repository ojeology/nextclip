Clarkesworld Magazine

Science fiction and fantasy short stories

Clarkesworld is open for original science fiction and fantasy of 1,000–22,000 words. Official rate is 14 US cents a word via PayPal, cheque or wire. Stories from all over the world and translations are welcome. AI-assisted work is banned. Simultaneous submissions are not allowed.

What they want

  • Well-written science fiction or fantasy. Dark SF/F is allowed. Horror as a genre is not.
  • Standard manuscript format, .RTF or .DOCX, via the online tracker only.
  • A cover letter that follows Neil Clarke’s published cover-letter guidance.

What they don't want

  • Email or postal submissions. Simultaneous submissions. Re-submitting a rejected story.
  • Another story within seven days of a rejection.
  • Work translated, written, developed or assisted by AI writing tools. Official: that can get you banned.
  • A long official list of hard sells includes zombies, sexy vampires, talking cats or swords, and pun-dependent humour. Read the page.

Requirements

  • Online tracker only
  • 1,000–22,000 words
  • No simultaneous submissions
  • No AI

Rights

First world electronic rights (text and audio), first print rights, and non-exclusive anthology rights for the annual Clarkesworld anthology. English-language unless stated otherwise.

How to submit

  • Read https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/submissions/.
  • Submit at https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/submissions_tracker/.
  • Keep your tracking number.
