African Arguments

Analysis, reportage and commentary on African current affairs

African Arguments relaunched in April 2026. Official write-for-us: they prioritise writers of African descent based on the continent, but are open to anyone who meets the standard. They have a limited budget and prioritise paying African journalists based in Africa. Fees vary. Average piece about 1,000 words.

What they want

  • Original angles on African politics, social issues, economics, development, gender, environment, culture — a story, not a topic.
  • Clear, evidenced, engaging writing for a pan-African, non-specialist audience.
  • Pitch or full draft. Tell them who you are, why now, how you will report it, and why you are the person.
  • April 2026 official relaunch: contact africanarguments@soas.ac.uk.

What they don't want

  • Vague general pitches.
  • Do not treat payment as guaranteed. Do not invent a naira or dollar fee.
  • Do not submit the same piece to both the main site and Debating Ideas.

Requirements

  • Email africanarguments@soas.ac.uk
  • Who you are, why now, reporting plan, clips if you have them
