Words Without Borders

Book reviews

Words Without Borders closed general submissions on 10 August 2026. Official exception: book-review pitches for titles at least four months from publication. Official review fee is $175. Email submissions@wordswithoutborders.org with subject “Book review”.

Official current rates: book reviews $175. Authors and translators are paid equally on translated writing. Fiction/nonfiction $150 per 1,000 words capped at $350, poetry $50–$75, interviews $175 — those doors are closed as of 10 August 2026 and are not this card.

What they want

  • A pitch for a book with a publication date at least four months in the future.
  • Subject line: Book review.

What they don't want

  • General fiction, poetry or nonfiction while the 10 August 2026 close is in force. Official: those submissions will not be considered or answered.

Requirements

  • Email submissions@wordswithoutborders.org
  • Book at least four months from publication

Rights

Translators hold copyrights to their translations. Review rights are not separately spelled out.

How to submit

  • Read https://wordswithoutborders.org/submissions/.
