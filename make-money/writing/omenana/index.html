Speculative fiction, essays and reviews

What they want

  • Speculative fiction and art (fantasy, science fiction, horror or magical realism) involving characters, settings or themes directly related to the African continent.
  • English (translations welcome). Original, previously unpublished. No fan fiction.
  • Non-fiction essays or reviews that deal with African speculative fiction.
  • When a window is open: one email attachment (.doc, .docx, .rtf or .odt) to editor@omenana.com, plus a cover letter with legal name, address, email, phone, brief publication history, bio (max 100 words) and a profile photo.

What they don't want

  • Do not submit while the stated window is closed.
  • Poetry, drama or film scripts.
  • Simultaneous submissions — the official page says they no longer accept them.
  • Revised drafts of work already submitted, unless they ask.
  • Art already published anywhere other than your portfolio, unless they request it.

Requirements

  • Wait for a new official window
  • Africa / African diaspora eligibility
  • Speculative, Africa-related
  • Standard manuscript format

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official submissions page.

How to submit

  • Do not treat the March 2026 window as open.
  • When a new window is posted on https://omenana.com/omenana-submissions/, email editor@omenana.com as specified there.
