Himal Southasian

Long-form journalism, essays and analysis on Southasia

Himal Southasian is open to pitches from writers across Southasia and the world. Official: they have set rates, and the standard rate is told as part of a commission. A public dollar figure is not on the current guidelines. AI-generated content is treated as plagiarism.

Official: Himal has set rates of remuneration; the final sum is based on length and nature. The standard rate is communicated as part of any commission or offer of publication. Older third-party pages quoted $100–$150. That figure is not on the current official guidelines and is not copied here.

What they want

  • Regional or cross-border Southasia stories, or very local stories told with a regional perspective.
  • A 300–600 word pitch: argument or narrative plus a tentative outline. Up to three previous-work links.
  • Completed articles may be attached. Drafts as Word documents, not PDFs or Google Doc links.
  • Occasional original short fiction — send a completed draft.

What they don't want

  • News or weekend-supplement features. Academic jargon for specialists.
  • Previously published work — official: without exception.
  • AI chatbots, writing tools or auto-generative systems used to produce content or analysis. Official: treated as plagiarism.

Requirements

  • Email editorial@himalmag.com
  • Original unpublished work
  • Southasia relevance

Rights

Copyright of the published article remains with Himal unless otherwise agreed.

How to submit
