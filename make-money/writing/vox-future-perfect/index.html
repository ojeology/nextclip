Vox

Future Perfect

Vox’s official How to pitch page currently accepts Future Perfect freelance pitches. Most stories run 1,500–2,500 words. Email futureperfectfreelance@vox.com. Rates are not published as a fixed number — official language is competitive, based on the work.

What they want

  • The world’s biggest, most neglected moral and technological problems.
  • Official core areas: global poverty and public health; catastrophic and existential threats; animal welfare and factory farming; progress studies; AI and biotechnology; philanthropy and altruism; moral philosophy.
  • A couple of paragraphs: unique angle, why now, why you, pre-reporting and source access, proposed headline and word count, a line about yourself, links to similar published work. All in the email body.

What they don't want

  • Attachments. AI-written pitches.
  • Q&As unless the subject is hard to get.
  • Traditional reviews. Shopping roundups. Manuscripts or academic papers.
  • Already-written pieces — they rarely accept them.

Requirements

  • Email futureperfectfreelance@vox.com
  • Pitch in the body, no attachment
  • Headline, word count, clips

Rights

Not fully stated on the pitch page. Assigned work goes through a Shortlist agreement.

How to submit

  • Read https://www.vox.com/how-to-pitch-vox-pitching-guidelines.
  • Email futureperfectfreelance@vox.com with the pitch in the body.
