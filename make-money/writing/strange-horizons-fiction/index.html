Strange Horizons

Speculative fiction

Strange Horizons pays 10 US cents a word (minimum $60) for original speculative fiction up to 10,000 words. Official page updated 9 August 2026: currently closed; they anticipate opening again in fall 2026. Worldwide. Author-only translations only. AI/LLM stories are not considered.

What they want

  • Good speculative fiction, including work from or about diverse and under-represented perspectives, written without exoticising.
  • When open: Moksha only. Previously unpublished in English.

What they don't want

  • Do not submit while the page says closed.
  • Simultaneous or multiple submissions. Reprints previously published in English. Resubmissions.
  • Stories translated or written using LLMs/AI.
  • Translations by someone other than the original author — those go to sister magazine Samovar.

Requirements

  • Wait for the fall 2026 reopen
  • Moksha
  • Speculative
  • No AI

Rights

First-printing world exclusive English-language rights including audio for six months. After that you may republish. Archive removal allowed after one year.

How to submit

  • Do not submit now.
  • Watch https://strangehorizons.com/wordpress/submit/fiction-submission-guidelines/.
