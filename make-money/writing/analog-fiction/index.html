Analog Science Fiction and Fact

Science fiction stories

Analog pays 8–10 US cents a word for short fiction up to about 20,000 words. Official: any writer, solely on merit. Science or technology must be integral to the plot. No simultaneous submissions. AI-assisted work is banned.

Payment conditions
Official fiction rate only: 8–10 cents per word for short fiction up to approximately 20,000 words. Serials (40,000–80,000 words) are 6 cents a word — that is a different length class, not copied as the short-fiction rate. Payment is on acceptance.

What they want

  • Science fiction in which some aspect of future science or technology is so integral that the story would collapse without it.
  • Strong, realistic stories with believable people (who need not be human).
  • Standard manuscript format via analog.magazinesubmissions.com.

What they don't want

  • Email or fax submissions. Simultaneous submissions.
  • Any submission written, developed or assisted by AI writing tools. Official: that can get you banned.
  • Stories that would still stand if you removed the science.

Requirements

  • Online system preferred
  • No simultaneous submissions
  • No AI

Rights

Official: First English Language serial rights plus certain non-exclusive rights explained in the contract (archive, translation, anthology, limited paper-goods excerpts).

How to submit

  • Read https://analogsf.com/contact-us/writers-guidelines/.
  • Submit fiction at https://analog.magazinesubmissions.com/.
  • Query analogsf@mustreadbookspublishing.com only after four months.
