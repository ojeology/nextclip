Griffith Review

Bodies of Work — fiction and non-fiction

Griffith Review’s Bodies of Work edition is open for fiction and non-fiction up to 4,000 words until 23 August 2026 (AEST). Official print rate is AUD$0.75 per word. Most writers are Australian; some are from overseas. That is not a closed country list. Disclose any generative AI.

What they want

  • Non-fiction and fiction that responds to the Bodies of Work theme — the human body in a digital, biohacking, looksmaxxing age; disability and gender; historic misconceptions.
  • Erudite yet accessible writing for a general reader. British/Australian spelling will be applied in-house.

What they don't want

  • Work after 23 August 2026 AEST.
  • Undisclosed generative AI. If you use AI-assisted technologies for creative purposes, disclose it in the cover letter. A statement will run with the published work.

Requirements

  • Submittable
  • ≤4,000 words
  • Responds to the theme
  • Disclose AI if used

Rights

Not fully quoted on the For Writers page. Successful print pieces go through a publishing contract.

How to submit

  • Read https://www.griffithreview.com/for-writers/.
  • Submit at https://griffithreview.submittable.com/submit before 23 August 2026 AEST.
