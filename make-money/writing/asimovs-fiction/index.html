Asimov’s Science Fiction

Science fiction short stories

Asimov’s pays 8–10 US cents a word for short stories up to 7,500 words, then 8 cents a word after that. Official page: any writer, previously published or not. No simultaneous submissions. AI-assisted work is banned.

Payment conditions
Official: 8–10 cents per word for short stories up to 7,500 words, and 8 cents for each word over 7,500. They seldom buy stories shorter than 1,000 words or longer than 20,000 words. Poetry is a separate $1-a-line rate, not copied onto this card.

What they want

  • Character-oriented science fiction. Official: SF dominates; they also buy some borderline fantasy, slipstream and surreal fiction.
  • Serious, thoughtful, accessible fiction; humour is allowed.
  • Standard manuscript format (.DOC, .DOCX or .RTF) via the online form.

What they don't want

  • Sword and sorcery. Explicit sex or violence.
  • Reprints. Simultaneous submissions.
  • Email submissions.
  • Any submission written, developed or assisted by AI writing tools. Official: that can get you banned.

Requirements

  • Online system only
  • No simultaneous submissions
  • No AI
  • Standard manuscript format

Rights

Official Must Read Magazines rights: exclusive first English-language publication rights for one year; then non-exclusive archive, translation, anthology and limited paper-goods excerpt rights. Major award anthologies may be licensed by the author without asking.

How to submit

  • Read https://asimovs.com/contact-us/writers-guidelines/.
  • Submit at https://asimovs.magazinesubmissions.com/.
  • Keep the tracking number. Query asimovs@mustreadbookspublishing.com only after 45 days.
