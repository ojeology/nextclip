The Lagos Review

Writing Loss & Grief

The Lagos Review is paying $250 per published essay (up to 1,500 words) on loss and grief. Rolling submissions June–December 2026. AI-produced essays and fiction are not considered.

What they want

  • Essays up to 1,500 words on loss and grief in all their forms — not only death.
  • Official examples include: loss of freedom through incarceration, kidnapping or injustice; migration and losing or searching for home; missed opportunities and vanished dreams; pregnancy loss; domestic or intimate partner violence; discrimination by race, tribe or ethnicity; losing a parent, child, partner, friend, or a stranger whose story pierced you.
  • First-person, honest, specific lived experience.

What they don't want

  • AI-produced essays will not be considered.
  • Fiction will not be considered for this call.

Requirements

  • Essay, maximum 1,500 words
  • Email to submissions@thelagosreview.ng

Rights

Not fully stated. The call notes that the top 12 published essays may be compiled into a book collection with a one-off $500 royalty. Broader rights terms are not on the call page.

How to submit

  • Email your essay to submissions@thelagosreview.ng.
  • Submissions are received on a rolling basis from June to December 2026.
