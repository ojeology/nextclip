The Tyee

News, features and first-person from British Columbia and Cascadia

The Tyee pays freelancers a day rate of $300 and usually assigns one- to three-day stories. Official focus is British Columbia, Cascadia and beyond. No country list. AI-written journalism is not published. If you hear nothing in seven business days, they are unlikely to take the pitch.

What they want

  • First-person that joins expertise and lived experience; counter-intuitive takes; critical analysis of a live controversy.
  • Subject line [PITCH]: headline. Angle, scene or quotes, hook, sources, why The Tyee, why you.
  • One editor only. Unsure which: editor@thetyee.ca.

What they don't want

  • A topic without an angle.
  • Journalism written or generated by AI.
  • Pitching more than one editor at the same time.

Requirements

  • One editor
  • Clear angle
  • No AI journalism

Rights

Not publicly stated on the submissions page.

How to submit

  • Read https://thetyee.ca/submissions/ and The Tyee’s AI policy.
  • Email one listed senior editor.
