Tech stories from outside the West

What they want

  • On-the-ground reporting connecting tech across places usually overlooked.
  • Official 2025 coverage areas still listed on the page: EV revolution; China outside China; US tech giants in emerging markets; innovation built outside the West.
  • Pre-reported pitch: headline, ≤300-word synopsis (angle, why now, what’s already been covered, length, delivery), and at least six named source types.

What they don't want

  • A pitch that is not pre-reported.
  • A made-up fee. The official pitch page does not state one.

Requirements

  • Pre-reported 300-word pitch
  • At least six sources listed
  • Send to the relevant editor on the team page

Rights

Not publicly stated on the pitch page.

How to submit

  • Use the template at https://restofworld.org/about/how-to-pitch-rest-of-world/.
  • Find the right editor at https://restofworld.org/about/team/ (Africa is listed under the Middle East and Africa editor).
