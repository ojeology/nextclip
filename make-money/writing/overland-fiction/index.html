Overland

Print and Friday Fiction

Overland’s official fiction page currently accepts stories only from writers living and/or working in or around Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand. Print pay is $580; online Friday Fiction is $180, plus 12% super. Nigeria does not qualify. Response can take up to six months.

What they want

  • Politically engaged, intellectually challenging, inventive fiction.
  • New work from emerging writers in regional Australia is actively sought.
  • Word document, 12pt Times New Roman, double-spaced. One story at a time.

What they don't want

  • Writers who do not live or work in or around Australia or Aotearoa New Zealand.
  • Novel excerpts. A second story while one is still under consideration.
  • Resubmitting an edited rejected story unless invited.

Requirements

  • Live or work in/around AU or NZ
  • One complete story
  • Submittable

Rights

Copyright remains with the authors. CAL copying income is split 80% author / 20% publisher.

How to submit

  • Read https://overland.org.au/submit/fiction-submissions/.
  • Use the subscriber or non-subscriber Submittable link on that page.
