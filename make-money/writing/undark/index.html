Undark Magazine

Science, society and reported features

Undark wants reported features, essays and op-eds where science collides with politics, economics and culture. Official door: the submissions form, not a general email. A fee is not published on the submissions or “what we’re looking for” pages. AI may not write pitches or drafts.

What they want

  • Stories where science intersects everyday life — vaccines, climate, agrochemicals, renewable energy, AI, research culture, money and bias in labs.
  • Use the form. Pitch meetings are generally Wednesdays 11 a.m. EST.
  • If you have not written for them: bio plus clips.

What they don't want

  • Emailing a pitch to a general inbox.
  • A made-up fee.
  • Pitches or drafts generated or line-edited by AI. Official exception only when AI itself is the subject, with disclosure.
  • Purely historical yarns, or first-person outside the op-ed silo unless your presence is essential. Climate/environment pitches are the most common and the hardest to land.

Requirements

  • Official form only
  • Clips if you are new
  • No AI pitch or draft

Rights

Not stated on the submissions page. Separate republishing guidelines exist for published Undark stories.

How to submit

  • Read https://undark.org/what-is-undark-looking-for/ and https://undark.org/ai-policy/.
  • Submit via the form on https://undark.org/submission-guidelines/.
