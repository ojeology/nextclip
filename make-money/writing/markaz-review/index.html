The Markaz Review

Essays, features, fiction and reviews from SWANA

The Markaz Review wants essays, features, fiction, memoir, poetry and reviews that amplify the Middle East, North Africa and Southwest Asia. Official: every contributor is paid an honorarium within 30 days of publication — the dollar amount is not on the submissions page. Comedy-issue deadline 1 September 2026; year-round pitches also invited. No AI.

What they want

  • Work from and about SWANA. Strong point of view, clear voice, authority, a commanding lead.
  • Comedy issue: fiction, nonfiction, poetry, hybrid — humorous writing or writing about humour. Deadline 1 September.
  • Query: one or two sentences on the story, why TMR, why you, plus clips if you are new.

What they don't want

  • AI-generated or AI-assisted text or images. Official: violators are permanently banned. Spellcheck/grammar tools are fine.
  • Stories or corrections in the body of an email — Word or Google Doc, track changes.
  • A made-up honorarium number.

Requirements

  • Duosuma or editor query
  • No AI
  • SWANA relevance

Not fully stated on the submissions FAQ.
