Afrolicious

Stories, essays, thinkpieces and rants

Afrolicious pays $75 for stories, essays, thinkpieces and rants of 600–800 words. Official eligibility is Black Africans and Black African diasporas.

What they want

  • Work at the intersection of the Africas and its diasporas. Afrolicious centers Blackness and Black Africanness.
  • Stories, essays, thinkpieces and rants of 600–800 words.
  • A pitch by email that includes: a 140-character thesis; one or two paragraphs describing the piece; three or four sources (potential sources count); a link to your website or blog; any ideas for the article design.
  • A personal website or blog outside social media is not required, but the publication says it prioritizes people who arrive with their own platform, regardless of size.

What they don't want

  • The official pitching page does not list genre exclusions beyond the formats it names.
  • AI policy is not stated on the official pitching page.

Requirements

  • Email pitch to hello@afrolicious.com
  • 140-character thesis
  • 1–2 paragraph description
  • 3–4 sources or potential sources
  • Link to website or blog
  • Optional article-design ideas

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official pitching page.

How to submit

  • Email hello@afrolicious.com with the pitch details listed above.
  • The official page says they will get back to you within two weeks.
