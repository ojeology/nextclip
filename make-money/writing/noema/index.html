Noema Magazine

Essays, reported features, interviews, fiction

Noema wants essays, reported features, interviews, video or short fiction. Official contact page: send a full draft, or a pitch of at least 500 words, to edit@noemamag.com. A dollar-per-word figure is not on the official contact page or the official pitch guidelines. Do not treat third-party $1/word claims as verified.

What they want

  • Longform journalism on ideas: AI, climate, democracy, capitalism, philosophy, geopolitics, technology, culture. Published by the Berggruen Institute.
  • A clear thesis, defended for the length of the piece. Subheadlines. Colourful opening, often a vignette with a specific person. Concrete examples. Solutions more than diagnosis.
  • Full draft preferred. Alternative: pitch of at least 500 words with a concise thesis, expected word count, and links to a few published pieces.
  • An AI statement in every pitch and draft: whether you used AI, and how, where and why.

What they don't want

  • Do not treat a third-party $1/word claim as official.
  • Undisclosed AI. Preference is for original work without AI; unauthorized or undisclosed use usually ends the relationship and forfeits the kill fee.
  • Sentences similar to your previously published or soon-to-be-published work.
  • Long mobile-unfriendly paragraphs. Posing questions you do not immediately answer.
