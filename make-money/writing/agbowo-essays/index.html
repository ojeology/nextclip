What they want

  • When open: essays 750–3,500 words, English (broken English acceptable), previously unpublished.
  • They look especially for essays rooted wholly or partly in an African context, and for uncommon topics, approaches and styles.
  • Word document, cover letter, 100–200 word bio, clear author photograph, social handles.
  • One entry per submission window.

What they don't want

  • Do not submit while the official page says submissions are closed.
  • Previously published essays.
  • Plagiarism or unacknowledged quotes.
  • More than one entry in a window.

Requirements

  • Wait for an official reopening announcement
  • 750–3,500 words
  • Word document + photo + bio

Rights

Not publicly stated on the official submissions page.

How to submit

  • Do not use the Google Forms on the page while the banner says submissions are closed.
  • Watch https://agbowo.org/submissions/ for the next window.
