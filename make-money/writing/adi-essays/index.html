What they want

  • Essays, when the window reopens on 1 October 2026. Adi publishes nonfiction (essays, interviews, reportage) as part of a literary magazine of global politics.
  • Interviews and translations are also listed as reopening 1 October 2026.
  • Fiction is listed separately: closed, reopening 8–30 November 2026 on Submittable, with official rates $500 (up to 5,000 words) and $200 (flash under 1,000 words). That is not this card.

What they don't want

  • Do not submit essays during the June–September 2026 staff break. The official page says they will not be able to reply.
  • AI-generated submissions or pitches.
  • Work previously published in English.
  • More than one Adi submission at a time.

Requirements

  • Wait until 1 October 2026
  • Re-read https://adimagazine.com/submissions/ before pitching — the method may be restated when the window opens

Rights

Adi acquires first exclusive, world English-language publication rights. Copyright remains with the author.

How to submit

  • Do not email an essay pitch before 1 October 2026.
  • On or after that date, use the official submissions page. The current page does not yet give the reopened essay pitch format.
