# Fresh Growth Hypotheses — surfaces we have never touched
**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Premise:** every visitor-acquisition effort so far is ONE channel — Google organic long-tail. We have gone *deeper* on it 3,237 pages. Everything below is a different surface, verified absent from the repo as of today (no social links, no newsletter, no llms.txt, no share/embed mechanics).

Each entry is a real hypothesis: the bet, why *this* site specifically, the cheapest possible test, the metric, and the kill criterion.

---

## H1 · Become the source AI assistants cite (GEO)
**The bet:** A growing share of "what does X mean" queries never reach a SERP — they die inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Gemini answers. Our entire catalogue is exactly the corpus those systems want to quote (plain-language explainers with named sources).
**Why us:** Copilot and ChatGPT's web search crawl through **Bing — the one engine we already actively feed** (IndexNow + 100/day API queue at 3,044). Nobody else in our niches publishes source-cited explainers at this scale. We are one `llms.txt` + fact-box pass away from being the easiest page for a model to quote.
**Cheapest test:** add `llms.txt` + per-desk `llms-full` digests; add a 3-bullet quotable fact box atop the 100 strongest explainers; verify each appears in Bing. One day of work.
**Metric:** referrals from chatgpt.com / perplexity.ai / copilot in analytics; brand citations when you ask the models our Tier-A questions.
**Kill if:** zero AI-engine referrals after 60 days.

## H2 · Pinterest: the second search engine nobody competes on
**The bet:** Pinterest is a visual search engine where pins compound for *years* (a tweet dies in hours, a pin in years). "Sinking funds", "APR vs APY", "deload week", "weighted vest" are real Pinterest searches with weak supply.
**Why us:** Money + Fitness + Home are Pinterest's three core categories — and those are three of our desks. OG images are already generated for every page (`build-og-images.py`), so each of the 3,237 pages is already pin-ready. Zero code required, ever.
**Cheapest test:** one account, 6 boards (save-money / fitness-form / home-maintenance / racket-sports / movie-night / desk-setup), 30 pins from existing pages over 2 weeks.
**Metric:** Pinterest Analytics outbound clicks.
**Kill if:** <500 monthly outbound clicks after 90 days.

## H3 · Turn the writers-desk dataset into the link-magnet (earned authority)
**The bet:** Rankings at our scale stop being limited by pages and start being limited by *authority*. The single fastest legitimate authority source is journalists citing original data.
**Why us:** The writers desk holds a genuinely proprietary dataset — **142 publications, human-verified, with pay rates, word counts, eligibility and dated checks**. Nobody publishes this cross-comparably. "The State of Paid Writing 2026: what 142 magazines actually paid" (median rate per word, who's open to international writers, who actually pays on time) is a chart-pack writers' media will cite for a year. Every citation lifts all 3,237 pages.
**Cheapest test:** one report page + 5 charts; answer 10 HARO/Qwoted journalist queries a month citing it.
**Metric:** new referring domains (free backlink checkers); organic CTR lift site-wide.
**Kill if:** <5 new referring domains after one full outreach cycle.

## H4 · One shareable artifact > a hundred more pages (product-led loop)
**The bet:** Every page we ship has a viral coefficient of ~0 — nothing on the site produces a shareable *object*. One tool that outputs a result card (image → WhatsApp/X/Reddit) creates traffic we don't pay Google for.
**Why us:** We already build calculators (48 writers tools, 5 money calculators) — the DNA exists, the share design never did. Candidates matched to obsessional communities: **darts checkout trainer** (501 crowd), **Hyrox time predictor** (fastest-growing sport, race-weekend spikes, data-nerd community), **"which movie era is your taste"** (1,578-page entertainment desk behind it).
**Cheapest test:** one tool (Hyrox predictor is the timely one — season runs through spring), result card with OG image + site watermark.
**Metric:** shares per 100 visits; referral sessions to the tool URL.
**Kill if:** <2% of visitors generate a share after one race weekend.

## H5 · Be the answer where the question already lives
**The bet:** Our own research documented that the SERPs for our target queries are **forum-dominated** — r/darts, r/pickleball, r/HYROX, AVSForum for the soap-opera effect. Those threads ARE the demand; a genuinely helpful answer with one link is a permanent referral + link.
**Why us:** We have the answers written and verified already — this is distribution of inventory, not content creation.
**Cheapest test:** 10 genuinely useful answers in 2 weeks from one real account (never spam; answer first, link once, disclose).
**Metric:** referral hits from those threads; movement of the linked page in Google for its query.
**Kill if:** answers get removed/ignored at >50% rate.

## H6 · The event calendar (freshness spikes + Discover eligibility)
**The bet:** We have never published for a moment. Oscars night, a Hyrox championship weekend, a title fight, a season premiere, Black Friday — each is a predictable traffic spike where a 24-hour-fast explainer ("how Oscars voting works", "how the fight is scored") wins the day and then evergreens.
**Why us:** The living-desk cadence machinery already tracks freshness; the Tier-B backlog is full of event-adjacent explainers waiting for exactly this trigger. Google Discover only surfaces fresh, event-relevant content — this is the door.
**Cheapest test:** 4 pre-planned event pages this quarter, each live *before* the event.
**Metric:** GSC Discover impressions; day-of traffic spike vs desk baseline.
**Kill if:** no measurable spike on any of the 4.
