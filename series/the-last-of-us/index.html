About The Last of Us

HBO's adaptation of the beloved video game. Joel, a broken smuggler, must escort Ellie, a teenage girl immune to the fungal pandemic, across a ruined America. Craig Mazin (Chernobyl) and the game's creator Neil Druckmann co-wrote it.

Notes

  • The infected are based on a real fungus that controls ants' brains.
  • Pedro Pascal and Bella Ramsey barely knew the game before auditioning.
  • The mushroom tendrils are practical effects, not CGI.
  • The show cost over $100 million — one of the most expensive TV seasons ever.
