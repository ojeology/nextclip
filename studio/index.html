The Writing Studio

Draft here. Nothing leaves this browser.

A quiet room for writing: drafts save themselves on this device as you type, with live word counts, a session goal, and one-click export. No account, no upload, no sync — your words are yours, locally.

How the Studio works

  • Autosave, locally. Every pause in typing saves the draft to this browser’s storage. No server holds a copy — which also means clearing your browser data clears your drafts, so export anything you care about.
  • Session goal. Set a word target; the meter fills as you write. Small, finished sessions beat rare heroic ones.
  • Focus mode hides the rest of the site. The keyboard shortcut Ctrl/Cmd+S saves on demand.
  • When the draft is ready, take it to the Tool Chest — the reading-time counter for a trim, the pitch checker before you submit, and the submission tracker when it’s gone out the door.
