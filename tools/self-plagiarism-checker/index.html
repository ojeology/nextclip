About this tool

How to use the repetition & self-plagiarism checker.

What it does. Checks your text for repeated or near-identical sentences within itself.

How to use it. Paste your text to find internal duplication. Review the flagged repeats and vary or remove them.

Why it matters. Accidental repetition reads as careless. Catching it within a single document is a fast, private quality check.
