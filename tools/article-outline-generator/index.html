About this tool

How to use the article outline generator.

What it does. Turns an idea into a structured article outline with an introduction, body sections and a conclusion.

How to use it. Enter your topic and the tool builds a skeleton you can rewrite into a full draft. Adjust sections to your argument and fill them in.

Why it matters. An outline removes the hardest part of starting. A clear structure means each paragraph has a job, so you write faster.
