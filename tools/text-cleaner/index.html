About this tool

How to use the text cleaner.

What it does. Removes double spaces, stray line breaks and other messy formatting from pasted text.

How to use it. Paste messy text and get clean, consistent text back with normal spaces and breaks. Useful when you copy from an email, a PDF or a website.

Why it matters. Copied text is rarely clean. A cleaner saves you from manually fixing spacing and line breaks before you submit or publish.
