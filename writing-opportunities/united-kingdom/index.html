Writing opportunities in United Kingdom

21 publications are based in United Kingdom, and 83 in total confirm in their own guidelines that they are open to writers from here. A further 42 do not state an eligibility rule either way.

Pay below is shown exactly as each publication states it. For scale, US$100 is about £74 at the mid-market rate of 2026-09-05. BRYME never converts a stated rate — it only shows an approximation.

A listing is an invitation to pitch, never a promise of acceptance or payment. Every entry links to the publication’s own guideline and the date BRYME last checked it.

House style when you write for United Kingdom

Getting these wrong will not sink a good idea, but they are the fastest way for an editor to tell you have not read them.

Spelling-ise and -our: organise, colour, honour — though Oxford University Press and most UK academic journals use -ize
Dates3 April 2026 — day first, no commas
CV or résuméCV, one to two pages, never with a photo
QuotationSingle quotes are common, and punctuation goes outside unless it belonged to the quotation

UK rates are usually quoted per 1,000 words rather than per word. Several UK markets charge a small submission fee.
