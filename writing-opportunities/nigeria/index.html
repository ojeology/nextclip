Publications based in Nigeria that pay writers, researched by hand with their stated pay, word count, eligibility and official guideline.

Nigerian publications pay in naira and usually require a Nigerian bank account. Fees are smaller in absolute terms than US or UK markets; they are also far less competitive, reply faster, and are the most realistic first published credit for a writer based in Nigeria.
